Alexis Notebook and Pen Gift Set

PLU 116691

Trendy gift set which is presented in a smart black gift box. It includes a medium size (approximately A5) Alexis Notebook and a retractable aluminium Centra Pen. The notebook has an attention-grabbing hard cover which is finished with a stylish combination of heather style PU matched with leather-look white PU. It has 80 leaves (160 pages) of 80gsm lined cream paper, an elastic closure band and a bookmark ribbon. The pen has a matte anodised barrel and shiny chrome accents. It has a black refill with 800 metres of writing ink and the barrel laser engraves to an oxidised white colour.
