Malibu Premium Sunglasses - Bamboo

PLU 111939

Retail quality sunglasses with an impact-resistant polycarbonate frame and on-trend natural bamboo arms. They are fitted with CE standard 100% UV400 lenses which provide both UVA and UVB protection. They can be supplied in an optional Montego sunglass case for an additional cost. Bamboo is a natural material and because the plant is fast growing, it places less demands on natural resources and produces a material that is durable and versatile. Bamboo can also produce unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding.
