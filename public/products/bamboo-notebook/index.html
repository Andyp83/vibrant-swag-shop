Bamboo Notebook

PLU 116213

Unique medium-size (approximately A5) hard cover notebook with a bamboo veneer. It has 80 leaves (160 pages) of 70gsm lined cream paper and a pen loop. The cover laser engraves to a natural etch. Bamboo is a natural material and because the plant is fast growing, it places less demands on natural resources and produces a material that is durable and versatile. Bamboo can also produce unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding.
