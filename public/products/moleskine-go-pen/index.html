Moleskine Go Pen

PLU 118898

The retractable Go Pen from Moleskine is a constant note-taking companion and is crafted from high grade ABS. It has a unique side opening clip that attaches firmly and neatly to any Moleskine hard cover notebook or planner. The Go Pen has a Moleskine refill with 3500 metres of black ink and a 1.0mm ballpoint tip for a smooth writing experience. The Moleskine logo is subtly debossed on the clip. Please note this product cannot be supplied without the Moleskine logo.
