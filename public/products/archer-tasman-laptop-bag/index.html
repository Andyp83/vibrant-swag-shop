ARCHER Tasman Laptop Bag

PLU 128649

Designed for life in motion, the ARCHER Tasman Laptop Bag pairs urban style with everyday practicality. Slim and travel friendly it is made from water-resistant washed nylon and features water-resistant zips. The main compartment is padded for a laptop plus it has an additional zippered padded sleeve for documents or extra devices. External front and back zippered pockets provide extra storage, while the woven carry handles and adjustable woven shoulder strap with padding ensure comfort on the go. The padded gusset provides added protection for belongings while reinforced zippers provide structure and durability. Finished with a unique textured fabric, ARCHER branded lining, and presented with an ARCHER branded hangtag.
