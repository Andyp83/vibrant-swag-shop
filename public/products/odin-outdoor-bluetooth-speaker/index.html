Odin Outdoor Bluetooth Speaker

PLU 121665

Practical 20W outdoor Bluetooth speaker which is compatible with most mobile devices that support Bluetooth. Manufactured from ABS and thermoplastic polyurethane, Odin is IPX6 water-resistant and is perfect to take on all adventures. Featuring an ambient light function with light display settings. It has a wireless connectivity range of over 10 metres and a playing time of approximately 4 hours depending on the volume. It is easily recharged from a laptop, a power bank or a USB mains adapter and it is supplied with a Type-C USB cable and an instruction manual.
