Keepsake Merchant Wine Cooler Bag

PLU 126973

This classically designed, thick cotton canvas 6.5L wine cooler bag is the perfect addition to any summer picnic. With on-trend natural tones this wine cooler offers both style and sophistication. The bag is wide enough to fit two standard wine bottles and features a conveniently located front pocket. It also has two carry handles one with an ergonomic dome-fastened grip, and the other a detachable and adjustable shoulder strap. The water-resistant PEVA/aluminium foil inner liner is easy to wipe clean, and the twin zip provides a wide opening for ease of use. The bag is presented with a Keepsake hangtag.
