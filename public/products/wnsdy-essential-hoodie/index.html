WNSDY Essential Hoodie

PLU 128475

Relaxed in fit and refined in finish, the Essential Hoodie is crafted from heavyweight 320gsm brushed fleece, blending 80% mercerised cotton with 20% recycled polyester for a soft yet structured feel. It features a cross-front, cord-free lined hood, cover-stitched hem and cuffs, and WNSDY's signature bar tacks at the back neck. Finished with a woven logo label and presented with a branded hangtag, it's built for comfort and made to last. Not only are they B Corp certified, 1 dollar from every WNSDY garment purchased is donated to We Are Mobilise, an organisation providing frontline support for people sleeping rough across Australia. They also personally fund and distribute street support packs for those in need. WNSDY - where great merch meets thoughtful impact.
