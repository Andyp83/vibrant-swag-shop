Horizon Duffle Bag

PLU 107665

Contoured duffle bag which is manufactured from strong 600D polyester with attractive patterned ripstop polyester accents and distinctive coloured piping. It has woven carry handles with a Velcro cuff and a padded woven shoulder strap which is adjustable and removable. Horizon features three external zippered compartments, one of which is an extended water-resistant inner bag for shoes or wet clothing. Other features include an adjustable water bottle holder, a reinforced base with plastic feet and an outer mesh pocket.
