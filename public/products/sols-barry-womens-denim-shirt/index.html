SOLS Barry Women's Denim Shirt

Fashion forward and functional, the Barry Women's Denim shirt is manufactured from 170gsm raw denim and is tailored for a comfortable and flattering fit. Darts on the chest and back give the shirt shape and the denim material makes this shirt well-suited for retail and hospitality workplaces. This shirt is a European fit and is available in six sizes. Washing it with similar colours and before first use is recommended.
