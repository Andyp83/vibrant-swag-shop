Aquarelle Watercolour Bookmark Pad

The Aquarelle Watercolour Bookmark Pad offers a creative way to personalise bookmarks. Each page has a pre-printed design ready to colour in using the included brush and six watercolour paint squares. Simply wet the brush to activate the colour and create a masterpiece. With twelve tear off pages and a kraft cover, it's a fun, compact activity for readers, crafters or anyone who enjoys simple creative moments.
