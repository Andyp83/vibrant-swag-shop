Lumos Bluetooth Speaker

PLU 119573

Extra-large deluxe 5W Bluetooth speaker which is compatible with all mobile devices that support Bluetooth. It is well suited for outdoor use and can also be used with an audio cable. The speaker has a colourful ambient light function with eight light display settings, a built-in microphone for phone calls, a silicone carry handle and music playback that can be controlled directly from the speaker. Lumos really attracts attention with its unique ability to brilliantly light up a brand. The artwork is laser engraved into the top of the speaker and illuminates when the light mode is on. Playing time is up to three hours and the speaker is easily recharged from a laptop, a power bank or a USB mains adapter. Lumos is presented in a black gift box with a Type-C cable, and an instruction leaflet.
