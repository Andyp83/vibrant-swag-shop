The Ember Rechargeable Table Lamp is a compact and stylish lighting solution for any occasion. Its rechargeable battery (Type-C) provides up to 6 hours of illumination on a single charge. Crafted from recycled ABS, the lamp is ideal for desks, bedside tables, or any space needing functional lighting. Presented in a natural gift box with product branding.
