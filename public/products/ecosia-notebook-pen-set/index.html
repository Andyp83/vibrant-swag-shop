Ecosia Notebook & Pen Set

PLU 117838

Medium-size (approximately A5), environmentally-focussed notebook with a hard cover that is finished with a stylish combination of natural cork and heathered cotton. The notebook has 80 leaves (160 pages) of 70gsm lined cream paper and an elastic pen loop which holds a matching retractable ball pen. The pen features a natural cork barrel and the accents are manufactured from a 50/50 blend of wheat straw fibre and BPA-free polypropylene. It has a black refill with 700 metres of German manufactured Dokumental ink and a tungsten carbide ball for improved writing quality. The set is presented in a natural unbleached cardboard sleeve.
