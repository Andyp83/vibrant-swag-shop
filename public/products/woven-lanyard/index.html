Woven Lanyard

PLU 110789

Premium 20mm wide lanyard which features the branding woven into a fabric strip in up to two PMS colours and then sewed onto a polyester backing. The fabric strip and the woven logo can be produced in most PMS colours. The polyester backing is available in 14 standard colours and PMS colours are available on request for a minimum of 1000pcs. PMS colour matching is approximate. When ordering choose our standard attachment configuration which includes a single metal clip and a safety clip or add any of our optional extra attachments as required. A production lead time of 20 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
