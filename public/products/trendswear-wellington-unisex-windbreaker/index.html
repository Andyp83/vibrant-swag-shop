The Wellington Unisex Windbreaker is made from lightweight yet durable 80gsm polyester ripstop with an internal TPU coating. The fabric is OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified, having been independently tested for harmful substances and proven safe for skin contact. Available in eight Australasian sizes along with the choice of coloured zipper pullers from the standard colour range.
