Calico Cooler Bag

PLU 115700

Large 27L cooler bag which also makes a great beach bag. It has PE foam insulation encased in a water-resistant PEVA inner liner. The outer construction is heavy 400gsm unbleached calico with long carry handles so it can be carried on a shoulder. Other features include a zippered top closure and a calico slip pocket on the front.
