Arcadia Picnic Backpack

PLU 112790

Superb four person picnic backpack which is manufactured from poly-canvas that has an on-trend heather style fabric finish. It has a large zippered cooler compartment at the front which is lined with thick PE foam insulation encased in a water-resistant PEVA inner liner. The rear compartment has a zip around closure that allows it to open out to 90 degrees revealing plates, cutlery, napkins and glasses for four people. There is also a wine waiter's knife and salt and pepper shakers. Other features include an external zippered pocket on the back, a padded front for comfortable carrying, adjustable shoulder straps and a carry handle.
