Sublime 10W Bluetooth Speaker

PLU 121393

Large 10W Bluetooth speaker which is compatible with most mobile devices that support Bluetooth. Manufactured from bamboo with a heather polyester front, Sublime is the perfect addition to any setting. It has a wireless connectivity range of over 10 metres and a playing time of approximately 6 hours depending on the volume. It is easily recharged from a laptop, a power bank or a USB mains adapter and it is supplied with a Type C cable and an instruction leaflet. Bamboo is a natural material which produces unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding.
