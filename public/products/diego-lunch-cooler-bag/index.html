Diego Lunch Cooler Bag

PLU 115271

Large 13L personal cooler bag which is ideal for keeping lunches and drinks cool. It has PE foam insulation encased in a water-resistant PEVA inner liner. The outer construction is 600D polyester with a base gusset for added capacity. Other features include long woven handles so it can be carried on a shoulder, a large slip pocket on the front and a zippered top closure.
