Hanford Cap

Fully customisable six-panel cap which is made from cotton twill and features an embroidered design on the peak and piping details on both the peak and cap. The colour of the cap fabric and the embroidery feature can be customised using colours from the standard colour palette. Other features include embroidered eyelets, a pre-curved peak, a sweatband and an adjustable snap closure which allows one size to fit most people. A production lead time of 35 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
