Fairbanks Custom Knitted Scarf

PLU 117534

Fully customised jacquard knitted acrylic scarf with tassels. The scarf can be knitted in up to four PMS colours at no extra cost and up to two of these colours can be used to make the tassels. PMS colour matching is approximate and the custom artwork is applied to both sides of the scarf. Embroidery is available for an additional charge. A production lead time of 40 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
