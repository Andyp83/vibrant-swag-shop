CamelBak Podium Bike Bottle - 700ml

PLU 118936

Stay hydrated wherever the trail takes you with the superb Podium 700ml drink bottle which has been designed by the CamelBak team to fit most bicycle bottle holders. The bottle is made primarily from TruTaste polypropylene, a proprietary blend of polypropylene that is food-safe and taste-free and the easy squeeze construction ensures maximum hydration with minimal effort. Podium has a secure screw on lid that features a high-flow, self-sealing drinking valve that maximises water flow and eliminates spills. It is easily removed for cleaning and has a locking mechanism to prevent leaks when the bottle is not in use. Podium is backed by the Got Your Bak lifetime guarantee. This product is not dishwasher safe and handwashing is recommended.
