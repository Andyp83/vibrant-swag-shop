Scribe Full Colour Note Pad - Medium

Fully customised A5 size, top opening notebook with silver wiro binding. It has 60 leaves (120 pages) of 70gsm cream paper which is available lined or unlined. A production lead time of 35 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
