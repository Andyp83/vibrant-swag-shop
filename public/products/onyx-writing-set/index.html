Onyx Writing Set

Luxurious notebook and pen gift set with matte black theme, beautifully presented in a black gift box. The hard cover medium-size (approximately A5) notebook has 80 leaves (160 pages) of unique black 110gsm unlined paper, a thermo PU cover, an elastic closure and a bookmark ribbon. The metal twist-action ballpoint pen has a lacquered matte black barrel, metal trim and 130m of silver gel ink. The Onyx Writing set also includes three spare pen refills.
