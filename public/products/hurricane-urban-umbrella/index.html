Hurricane Urban Umbrella

The Urban umbrella is a functional and fashion-forward umbrella which provides protection from the elements. The vented eight-panel pongee canopy is supported by 72cm fibreglass ribs and a strong aluminium shaft with automatic opening. Urban has a rubberised handle and a matching hurricane branded pongee carry sleeve with an adjustable shoulder strap.
