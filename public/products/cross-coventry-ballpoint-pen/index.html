Cross Coventry Ballpoint Pen

PLU 128610

Since 1846, Cross has been redefining the art of writing by creating distinguished, expertly crafted writing tools. The Cross Coventry ballpoint pen blends timeless design with exceptional craftsmanship. Featuring the iconic Cross design, signature conical top, and cap-over-barrel design, this pen boasts a sleek gloss black lacquer finish with polished chrome appointments. The swivel-action propel/repel mechanism and specially formulated ink provide a smooth, flawless writing experience and a Lifetime Mechanical Warranty gives you peace of mind. The Cross name engraved on the pen clip adds a touch of distinction. Presented in a Cross branded gift box with a cardboard sleeve, this pen is the perfect gift for anyone who appreciates the art of fine writing. Additional colour options are available - please contact our customer service team for stock availability and pricing.
