Cross ATX Ballpoint Pen

PLU 128614

Since 1846, Cross has been redefining the art of writing by creating distinguished, expertly crafted writing tools. The Cross ATX ballpoint pen features modern industrial design with its matte black lacquer finish and polished chrome-plated appointments. Featuring a distinctive integrated clip, wide barrel, and elegantly tapered ends, the ATX is perfectly balanced for a superior writing experience. The swivel-action propel/repel mechanism and specially formulated ink provide a smooth, flawless writing experience and a Lifetime Mechanical Warranty gives you peace of mind. The Cross name engraved on the pen clip adds a touch of distinction. Presented in a Cross branded gift box with a cardboard sleeve, this pen is the perfect gift for anyone who appreciates the art of fine writing. Additional colour options are available - please contact our customer service team for stock availability and pricing.
