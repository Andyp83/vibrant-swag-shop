Xtra Silicone Wrist Band - Debossed

PLU 117055

Silicone wrist band with an extra branding spot for maximum impact. It has many uses and is ideal for fundraising, awareness campaigns, promotion and as admission bracelets for events. There are 14 standard colours and PMS colours are available on request. It is branded with debossing which gives a 3D effect below the surface. All or part of the debossed surface can be colour filled with two or more colours which is optional. One size fits all and they can be supplied in an optional polybag, with or without a full colour label. A production lead time of 25 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
