Bamboo Phone Holder

PLU 121717

Bamboo phone holder which sticks onto the back of any mobile phone and provides grip for using the device. It is manufactured from plastic and bamboo. The phone holder also works as a stand, and it will hold a mobile phone in a horizontal position on a surface for easy viewing. Bamboo is a natural material and because the plant is fast growing, it places less demands on natural resources and produces a material that is durable and versatile. Bamboo can also produce unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding.
