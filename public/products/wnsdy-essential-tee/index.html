WNSDY Essential Tee

PLU 128474

Made for comfort and built to last, this unisex tee is crafted from 100% organic cotton in a soft, breathable 180gsm single jersey. It is cut in a regular fit and finished with signature WNSDY bar tacks at the back neck for a considered touch. A woven WNSDY inside label and branded hangtag complete the look. WNSDY believes clothing can do more than just dress the body - it can create meaningful, real world change. Not only are they B Corp certified, 1 dollar from every WNSDY garment purchased is donated to We Are Mobilise, an organisation providing frontline support for people sleeping rough across Australia. They also personally fund and distribute street support packs for those in need. WNSDY - where great merch meets thoughtful impact.
