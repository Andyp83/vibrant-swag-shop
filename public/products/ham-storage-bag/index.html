Ham Storage Bag

PLU 111807

A handy drawstring bag for storing ham in the refrigerator. It is manufactured from 140gsm unbleached natural cotton and extends the life of a ham by reducing moisture loss and allowing it to breathe. Cotton is easy to wash, ensuring the bag can be reused many times. A branded Ham Storage Bag is the perfect way to present a gifted ham. It adds perceived value to the gift and the recipient can reuse the branded bag for many years to come.
