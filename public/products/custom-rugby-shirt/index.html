Custom Rugby Shirt

Comfortable and functional, this unisex custom rugby shirt is manufactured from 350gsm, 35% polyester and 65% cotton yarn knit, making this shirt well-suited for sporting teams and college leavers. Custom labels are available upon request free of charge.
