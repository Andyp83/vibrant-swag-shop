Beaumont Stone Paper Notebook

PLU 123013

The Beaumont notebook is a one-of-a-kind writing companion with a soft kraft cover. It has 80 leaves (160 pages) of 120gsm lined stone paper. This innovative paper stock is created by blending powdered limestone with bio-polythene resin, resulting in exceptionally durable, tear-resistant pages that are water, oil and grease-resistant and provide a luxuriously smooth writing surface. The medium-size (approximately A5) notebook also features an elastic closure, a pen loop and a bookmark ribbon.
