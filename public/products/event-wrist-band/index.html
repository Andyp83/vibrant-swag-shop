Event Wrist Band

Polyester wrist band that can be branded in full colour. The wrist band has a unique one-way toggle and once it is slid into place the only way to get the wrist band off is to cut it with scissors. It is ideal for entry to events as it is very visible and cannot be reused. A production lead time of 25 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
