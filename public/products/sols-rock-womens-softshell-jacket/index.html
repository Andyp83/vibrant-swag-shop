SOLS Rock Women's Softshell Jacket

PLU 120005

Premium 340gsm women's long sleeve jacket manufactured from 94 percent polyester with six percent elastane added for stretch comfort. This layer is windproof, breathable, and has a waterproof rating of 8000mm (waterproof in light conditions). It has 160gsm internal polyester padding and is lined with polyester taffeta which provides triple-layer protection from the elements. Rock has two large vertical zippered side pockets which double as hand warmers, a vertical zippered chest pocket, a large zippered internal pocket with an opening for headphones, and an internal mesh slip pocket. It has a longer back panel and an adjustable elastic waist closure with a locking toggle for additional warmth. Other features include adjustable Velcro cuff closures, inner stretch cuffs, a fully adjustable and removable fleece-lined hood with a peak, a zippered pocket on the left sleeve, and reflective piping on the side pockets and the back for added visibility. Rock is a European fit and is available in six sizes.
