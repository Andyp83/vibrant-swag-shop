Maverick Sunglasses - Bamboo

PLU 120342

Design inspired sunglasses with a polished ABS frame and natural bamboo arms. They are fitted with CE standard 100% UV 400 lenses that provide both UVA and UVB protection and can be supplied in an optional Montego sunglass case for an additional cost. Bamboo is a natural material and because the plant is fast growing, it places less demands on natural resources and produces a material that is durable and versatile. Bamboo can also produce unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding. These sunglasses comply with the ASNZS 1067-2016 standard.
