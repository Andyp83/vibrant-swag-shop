Demio Notebook and Pen Gift Set

PLU 116690

Stylish gift set which is presented in a smart black gift box. It includes a medium-size (approximately A5) Demio Notebook and a retractable aluminium Lancer Pen. The notebook has a flexible PU cover which thermo debosses to a stunning two-tone finish and features 112 leaves (224 pages) of colour-edged 80gsm cream paper with lines. The notebook includes a pen holder in the spine and a bookmark ribbon. The pen has shiny anodised coloured barrels and a matt anodised silver barrel, all of which have shiny chrome accents. It has a black large volume refill with 1200 metres of writing ink. Lancer laser engraves to an oxidised white colour.
