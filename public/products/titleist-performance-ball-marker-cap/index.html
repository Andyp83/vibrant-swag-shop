Titleist Performance Ball Marker Cap

PLU 118429

This high-performance Titleist cap offers style and functionality, living up to the brand's excellent reputation on the golf course. This outstanding six-panel cap is made from Titleist's moisture-wicking material, known for its odour-reducing and anti-microbial properties with UV sun protection. A magnet in the pre-curved peak holds a metal Titleist ball marker for easy access on the green. Other features include embroidered eyelets, a sweatband and an adjustable Velcro closure so one size fits most people. The cap is branded with the Titleist logo on the front. Please note this product cannot be supplied without the Titleist logo.
