Swiss Peak ANC TWS Earbuds

PLU 125277

High-quality Bluetooth earbuds from Swiss Peak offering high-quality stereo sound with active noise cancellation (ANC) capability. When ANC is enabled, these earbuds can provide up to 3.5 hours of music playback, and up to 4.5 hours with ANC disabled. Conveniently recharge the earbuds while on the go using the stylish 400mAh charging case, which features the Swiss Peak logo. Three different eartip sizes are included to ensure a comfortable fit for most users. Additionally, the earbuds are equipped with built-in controls for adjusting volume, selecting tracks, and handling calls. The earbuds are elegantly presented in a black gift box with Swiss Peak branding, making them an ideal gift for any music enthusiast.
