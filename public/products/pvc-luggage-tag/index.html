PVC Luggage Tag

PLU 110553

Soft and flexible PVC rubber luggage tag with a removable address label which offers the option of 3D on one side. This allows part or all of the artwork to be raised from the surface of the product for maximum impact. Pricing includes up to four PVC colours with more colours available for an additional charge. Both the luggage tag and the PVC strap are available in almost any colour but PMS colour matches are only approximate. A production lead time of 25 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
