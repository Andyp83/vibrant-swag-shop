SPICE Waste2Gear Weekend Sports Bag

PLU 126953

SPICE strive to create extraordinary products that have a positive impact on the planet. Their Waste2Gear range uses recovered ocean plastics, which are gathered, cleaned, and converted into fibres and woven into long-lasting fabric. This water-resistant weekender duffle bag is 100% carbon neutral to delivery and features a double-zippered main compartment with interior pockets for organisation, and a zippered front pocket to easily access belongings on the go. Perfect for travel, the bag has an adjustable padded shoulder strap, a luggage trolley pass through, and the choice of comfortable carry handles in two positions.
