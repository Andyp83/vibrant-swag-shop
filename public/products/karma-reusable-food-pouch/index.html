Karma Reusable Food Pouch

PLU 115228

Karma is a reusable food storage pouch with a secure Velcro closure which is designed to protect sandwiches, buns, rolls, bagels, biscuits etc. It eliminates the need for single-use plastic food wrap and ensures lunch boxes are waste-free. Karma has a hygienic food grade EVA liner which is water-resistant and can easily be wiped clean or washed in a washing machine. A production lead time of 30 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
