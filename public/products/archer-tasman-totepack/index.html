ARCHER Tasman Totepack

PLU 128647

Designed for life in motion, the ARCHER Tasman Totepack pairs urban style with everyday practicality. Made from water-resistant washed nylon and featuring water-resistant zips, it offers a 13L main compartment with a padded sleeve for devices and multiple internal compartments to keep your essentials organised. An external front zippered pocket provides additional storage, while the woven carry handles with padded cuff ensure carrying comfort. For added versatility, it converts from tote to backpack with concealed padded shoulder straps and easy hook attachment. The padded gusset provides added protection for belongings while reinforced zippers provide structure and durability. Finished with a unique textured fabric, ARCHER branded lining, and presented with an ARCHER branded hangtag.
