Origami Foldable Backpack

PLU 126518

Handy 16L backpack made from durable 210D ripstop polyester and features lightly padded and adjustable shoulder straps. When not in use, the backpack can be folded away into a small carry pouch, making it perfect for travel. The pouch is discreetly zipped away into the bag when the backpack is in use. This lightweight backpack has a large zippered main compartment, as well as two additional zippered pockets in the front.
