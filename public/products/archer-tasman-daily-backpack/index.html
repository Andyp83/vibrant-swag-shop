ARCHER Tasman Daily Backpack

PLU 128646

Designed for life in motion, the ARCHER Tasman Daily Backpack pairs urban style with everyday practicality. Made from water-resistant washed nylon and featuring water-resistant zips, it offers a 13L main compartment with a padded compartment for devices up to 16.5". Multiple internal pockets keep your tech essentials in place, while the two drink bottle holders mean you are ready for wherever life takes you. Additional padding in the gusset provides additional protection for your belongings. Adjustable padded shoulder straps and a carry handle make this backpack easy to wear all day. Finished with a unique textured fabric, ARCHER branded lining, and presented with an ARCHER hangtag.
