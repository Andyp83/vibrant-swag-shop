Harvard Notebook and Pen Gift Set

PLU 129680

A coordinated notebook and pen set with timeless style. The medium-sized (approximately A5) Harvard notebook is made almost entirely from recycled materials and features a textured PU hard cover, 80 leaves of 70gsm lined natural paper, and a matching elastic closure band, bookmark ribbon, and pen loop. It is paired with the Martini Pen, featuring a lacquered recycled aluminium barrel, shiny chrome trim, and 800 metres of black Dokumental ink for smooth, reliable writing. An optional paper belly band is available on request and can be branded in full colour. The notebook and pen set is presented in a black gift box.
