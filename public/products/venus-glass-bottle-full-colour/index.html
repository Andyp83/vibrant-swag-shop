Venus Glass Bottle - Full Colour

PLU 115845

Deluxe 600ml borosilicate glass drink bottle which has an insulating soft neoprene sleeve that keeps drinks cool for longer. The sleeve is accented with grey binding and it has a grey woven carry strap. Both the sleeve and base can be branded in spot colours or stunning full colour. Venus has a secure screw on stainless steel lid which can be laser engraved with a logo. Borosilicate is a lightweight glass that is exceptionally strong and shatter-resistant. Glass is a natural choice for a clean, pure taste and is a safe and reusable plastic alternative. This product is not intended to be used with carbonated or hot liquids and is not dishwasher safe. Handwashing is recommended. A production lead time of 25 working days applies to the sleeve by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
