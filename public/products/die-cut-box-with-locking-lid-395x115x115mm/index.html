Die Cut Box with Locking Lid - 395x115x115mm

PLU 126067

Premium custom gift box made from durable 1.5mm e-flute corrugated cardboard to provide reliable protection during transit. Designed to fit a magnum-sized wine bottle, this box is perfect for gifting and building strong brand recognition. Featuring a convenient hinged lid and a tuck and tongue closure, selected panels of the box can be customised with CMYK digital print. It is supplied flat-packed for easy postage and storage, and its self-locking design ensures hassle-free assembly. The assembled internal dimensions are 395mm x 115mm x 115mm (LxWxH).
