SPICE Waste2Gear Computer Bag

PLU 126950

SPICE strive to create extraordinary products that have a positive impact on the planet. Their Waste2Gear range uses recovered ocean plastics, which are gathered, cleaned, and converted into fibres and woven into long-lasting fabric. This water-resistant laptop bag is 100% carbon neutral to delivery and features a main zippered pocket with an internal padded laptop compartment, a zippered front pocket to easily access belongings on the go. Perfect for travel, the bag has an adjustable padded shoulder strap, a luggage trolley pass through and woven carry handles.
