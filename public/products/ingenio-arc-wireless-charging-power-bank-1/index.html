INGENIO Arc Wireless Charging Power Bank

The Arc Wireless Charging Power Bank blends elegance with performance, offering stylish power on the go. Designed to seamlessly integrate into everyday life, it can charge up to two devices simultaneously with a wireless output of up to 15W. In addition, it comes with a metal ring for non-MagSafe phones ensuring compatibility with any device. Additional features include LED power level and charging indicator lights, and a magnetic foldable stand that doubles as a non-slip base, offering hands-free convenience in both portrait and landscape positions. The sleek black glass finish, refined copper accents, and quality components make this compact and lightweight charger a standout accessory. Presented in a beautifully crafted INGENIO gift box, it is ideal for corporate gifting, and the ultimate brand statement. INGENIO - Designed to lead, built to last, made to inspire.
