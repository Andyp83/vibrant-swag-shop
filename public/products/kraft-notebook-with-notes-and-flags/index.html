Kraft Notebook with Notes and Flags

PLU 129579

The Kraft Notebook with Notes and Flags is a practical, all-in-one writing solution for everyday organisation. The wiro bound notebook features 80 leaves of lined recycled paper, complemented by integrated sticky notes and adhesive flags for quick referencing and reminders. A recycled kraft cardboard cover provides a clean, natural finish, and a matching kraft pen is included for added convenience.
