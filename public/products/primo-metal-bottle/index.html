Primo Metal Bottle

PLU 110753

Large 900ml stainless steel drink bottle which has a secure screw on lid with a flip cap that has the option of adding a smart resin coated logo on the top. The lid also has a carry handle and a silicone outer ring for improved grip. Primo is BPA-free and has a wide opening which is ideal for adding ice cubes or fruit for flavouring. The black and white bottles laser engrave to a shiny steel finish and the silver bottle engraves to a natural etch. This product is not dishwasher safe and handwashing is recommended.
