Titleist Tour Performance Cap

PLU 118398

Look and feel like a golf pro with the Titleist Tour Performance Cap. Made from moisture-wicking material which reduces odour, the cap offers anti-microbial technology and UV sun protection. The six panel cap has a pre-curved peak, embroidered eyelets for improved airflow and an absorbent sweatband. The Titleist logo is boldly branded on the front with stunning 3D embroidery and the adjustable stretch clasp closure allows one size to fit most people. The cap can be tastefully co-branded on one or both of the back panels. Please note this product cannot be supplied without the Titleist logo.
