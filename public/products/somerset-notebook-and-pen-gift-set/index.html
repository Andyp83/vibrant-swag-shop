Somerset Notebook and Pen Gift Set

PLU 129678

A coordinated notebook and pen set with a natural, modern look. The medium-sized (approximately A5) Somerset Notebook features a hard cover with a stylish cork finish, 80 leaves of lined cream paper, an elastic closure, and a bookmark ribbon. It is paired with the Inca Pen, featuring a natural cork barrel, soft-touch grip, and 1000 metres of black Dokumental ink for smooth, reliable writing. An optional paper belly band is available on request and can be branded in full colour. The notebook and pen set is presented in a natural gift box.
