ARCHER Tasman Travel Backpack

PLU 128651

Designed for life in motion, the ARCHER Tasman Travel Backpack pairs urban style with everyday practicality. Made from water-resistant washed nylon and featuring water-resistant zips, it offers a spacious main compartment that opens flat with elastic packing straps and multiple storage pockets to keep your essentials organised. A padded back compartment protects devices, while the padded gusset and reinforced zippers ensure durability. A cushioned pack panel and adjustable padded shoulder straps provide all-day comfort. Finished with unique textured fabric, ARCHER branded lining, and presented with an ARCHER branded hangtag.
