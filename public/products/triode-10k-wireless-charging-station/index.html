Triode 10k Wireless Charging Station

PLU 200321

Luxury phone charging station with a detachable 10,000mAh wireless power bank. It can be used as a stand for devices that are enabled for wireless charging or the power bank can be removed and used independently for portable wireless charging. The power bank can also be used as a conventional charger with a cable and it can charge two devices at once. It can be recharged simply by placing it back on the stand or by using a USB port or a mains adapter. Triode has an input power of 5V/2A, a wireless output of 5V/2.4A and a USB output of 5V/1A. It is presented in a white box with an 80cm Micro-B USB cable and an instruction leaflet.
