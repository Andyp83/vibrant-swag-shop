Swiss Peak 10W Bass Speaker

PLU 134192

The Swiss Peak 10W Bass Speaker delivers powerful sound in a compact package. Made from recycled aluminium and ABS, it features a dedicated subwoofer for extra punchy bass. The 1200mAh battery can be recharged via USB-C and provides up to five hours play time with a charging time of approximately three hours. Take your music with you thanks to an included carry strap and Bluetooth or AUX connectivity. Presented in a black gift box with Swiss Peak branding.
