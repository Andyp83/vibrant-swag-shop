Rollink Flex Earth Suitcase - Medium

PLU 126633

Rollink's Flex Earth Suitcase is the world's slimmest suitcase - this generous 60L case is perfect fortravel and folds down to an incredibly compact 8cm (11cm including wheels) when not in use. Belongings are protected via the lightweight yet durable polypropylene hard outer shell, which is scratch-resistant and water-resistant. The interior is tastefully lined with the Rollink logo and features foldaway structural panels for additional support. A telescopic handle and sturdy wheels make this suitcase perfect for regular travellers.
