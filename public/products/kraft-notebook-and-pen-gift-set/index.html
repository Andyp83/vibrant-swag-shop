Kraft Notebook and Pen Gift Set

PLU 129676

A coordinated notebook and pen set with a clean, natural look for everyday organisation. The medium-sized (approximately A5) notebook features a durable kraft cover, 80 leaves of lined cream paper made from sugarcane waste, an elastic closure band, and a pen loop. It is paired with a retractable Lancer Kraft Pen with a natural or black kraft paper barrel, shiny chrome trim, and 1200 metres of black Dokumental ink for smooth, reliable writing. The notebook and pen set is presented in a natural gift box.
