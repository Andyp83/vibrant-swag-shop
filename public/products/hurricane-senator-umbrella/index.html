Hurricane Senator Umbrella

PLU 200608

The Senator Umbrella is part of the premium Hurricane range and offers a generous canopy while folding down to 36cm. The eight-panel vented pongee canopy is supported by 68cm fibreglass ribs and a strong steel frame. Senator has automatic opening and closing, a rubberised handle and a matching pongee sleeve.
