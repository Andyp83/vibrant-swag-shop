Montague Picnic Backpack

PLU 128884

This practical two-person picnic backpack is crafted from durable 600D polyester and features an insulated, foil-lined main compartment to keep food and drinks cool. The front compartment holds cutlery, plates, and glasses for two, while an additional slip pocket offers extra storage and prime space for branding. Designed for comfort with padded, adjustable shoulder straps, it's the perfect hands-free solution for picnics in hard-to-reach locations.
