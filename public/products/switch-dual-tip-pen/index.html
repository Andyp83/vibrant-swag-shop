Switch Dual-Tip Pen

PLU 129218

The Switch Dual-Tip Pen offers the best of both worlds with an inkless metal alloy tip on one end and a smooth ballpoint pen on the other. The inkless tip deposits small amounts of metal onto the page, creating pencil-like marks without the need for sharpening or refills, while the ballpoint pen features a 1mm nib with black Dokumental ink and a 1000m writing distance. Available in matte black or gloss white, each pen includes removable plastic caps for both ends.
