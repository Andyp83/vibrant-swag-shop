Telescopic Straw with Case

PLU 116799

Reusable stainless steel straw with a removable silicone mouthpiece and cleaning brush. The straw telescopically folds down to fit inside its own clear carry tube which seals shut with a silicone stopper. This has an adjustable loop so the carry tube can be attached to a key ring or bag. Steel straws are a reusable alternative to single-use plastic straws and are a great way to send a positive message. Branding is on the case.
