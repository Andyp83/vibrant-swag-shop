Osprey Arcane Roll Top Backpack

PLU 122430

A beautifully crafted everyday bag, the Arcane Roll Top was designed to fit a variety of scenarios and withstand the demands of daily use. Pack it with the day's essentials in its main roll-top compartment and a 16-inch padded laptop sleeve. Built with durable certified recycled fabric, intuitive straightforward features, and a surprisingly comfortable carry with stretch mesh shoulder straps. Other features include a stretch water bottle side pocket, vertical zip front panel pocket and a front panel stash pocket. Arcane Roll Top is made from 12.5 plastic bottles.
