Pinnacle Distance Golf Ball

PLU 125559

Pinnacle's Distance Golf Ball offers top-tier performance at an affordable price point. Produced by the same company as Titleist golf balls, this ball offers quality without compromising price. The high energy, low compression core, soft cover, and advanced 332 icosahedral dimple design create low spin for longer shots and a soft feel. These quality golf balls are nicely presented in individual boxes in an outer box of one dozen balls. The branding on the packaging is subject to change without notice and this product must be ordered in dozens. Please note this product cannot be supplied unbranded or without the Pinnacle logo.
