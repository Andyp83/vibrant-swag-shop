Sophisticated 600ml borosilicate glass drink bottle which features an insulating soft neoprene sleeve that helps to keep drinks cool, and a trendy screw on bamboo lid. Both the sleeve and base can be branded in stunning full colour. Borosilicate is a lightweight glass which is exceptionally strong and shatter-resistant. Glass is a natural choice for a clean, pure taste and is a safe and reusable plastic alternative. A production lead time of 25 working days applies to the sleeve. This product is not dishwasher safe and handwashing is recommended.
