Bamboo Double Wall Cup

PLU 200297

Make a statement with this 350ml, nature-inspired double wall coffee cup which has a genuine bamboo outer wall. It has a stainless steel inner wall and a secure screw on lid with a splashproof flip closure. The cup will keep drinks hot or cold for longer and has smart stainless steel trim. It is BPA-free and is presented in a black gift box. This product is not dishwasher safe and handwashing is recommended. Bamboo is a natural material and because the plant is fast growing, it places less demands on natural resources and produces a material that is durable and versatile. Bamboo can also produce unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding.
