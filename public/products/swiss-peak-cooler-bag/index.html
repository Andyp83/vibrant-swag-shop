Swiss Peak Cooler Bag

PLU 116494

Deluxe 16L Swiss Peak Cooler Bag with thick PE foam insulation encased in a water-resistant PEVA inner liner. The outer construction features a stylish combination of 1680D and 600D polyester. It has strong woven carry handles with a Velcro cuff as well as an adjustable and removable woven shoulder strap with a shoulder pad. Other features include a large zippered top closure, a zippered pocket on the front and slip pockets on both sides.
