Genoa Notebook and Pen Gift Set

PLU 129681

A coordinated notebook and pen set with a refined, contemporary feel. The medium-sized (approximately A5) Genoa Notebook features a soft cover with a leather-look PU finish, 80 leaves of 80gsm lined cream paper, and a matching bookmark ribbon. It is paired with the Bravo Pen, featuring a matte lacquered recycled aluminium barrel and shiny chrome trim. A metal grip with rubber coating provides a comfortable soft-touch feel, while 800 metres of black Dokumental ink ensures smooth, reliable writing. The notebook and pen set is presented in a black gift box.
