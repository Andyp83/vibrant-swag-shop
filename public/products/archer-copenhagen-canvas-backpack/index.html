ARCHER Copenhagen Canvas Backpack

Designed for life in motion, the ARCHER Copenhagen Canvas Backpack is crafted from durable 450gsm washed canvas with matte PU accents and brushed metal fittings, combining modern utility with vintage-inspired style. The spacious 25L main compartment has a secure zippered closure, complemented by a magnetic flip closure for added protection and is finished with ARCHER branded lining for a refined touch. The backpack features a padded device sleeve keeps your tech safe, a front zippered pocket, and a side bottle holder for added convenience. Padded shoulder straps ensure comfortable carrying.
