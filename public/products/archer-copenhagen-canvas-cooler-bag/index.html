ARCHER Copenhagen Canvas Cooler Bag

PLU 128383

Designed for everyday, the ARCHER Copenhagen Cooler Bag pairs modern function with vintage-inspired style. Crafted from durable 450gsm washed canvas, it's finished with matte PU accents and brushed metal fittings. The spacious 14L main compartment is lined with aluminium foil to maintain temperature, making it perfect for lunches, picnics, or day trips. An external zippered pocket offers extra storage for small essentials. Woven carry handles and an adjustable shoulder strap ensure easy, comfortable carrying. Presented with an ARCHER branded hangtag.
