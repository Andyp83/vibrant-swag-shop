IdealCup - 470ml

PLU 126433

IdealCup is the first reusable coffee cup to be conceptualised and manufactured in New Zealand. This 470ml cup is manufactured from BPA-free polypropylene and features ergonomic fins around the cup to disperse heat, allowing the cup to be carried comfortably on the go. IdealCup's secure lid is manufactured from BPA-free polyethylene and sports a smart rotating sipper plug to ensure there are no leaks or spills. IdealCup is proud to have achieved the rigorous Environmental Choice New Zealand (ECNZ) tick of approval for their reusable cup - one of only two manufacturers in New Zealand to be granted the certification. Both the cup and lid use plastics that are made in New Zealand. IdealCup has a strong ethos surrounding their product, using effective product stewardship to ensure the IdealCup creates as little waste as possible. While each IdealCup is designed for a lifetime of reuse, every IdealCup can be returned to IdealCup HQ at the end of its life to be ground down and repurposed into new IdealCup products.
