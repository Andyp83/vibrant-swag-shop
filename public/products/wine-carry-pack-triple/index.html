Wine Carry Pack - Triple

PLU 126069

Custom wine presentation box made from durable 1.5mm e-flute corrugated cardboard with display windows. This box holds three standard wine bottles, perfect for gifting and building strong brand recognition. Featuring a convenient carry handle, the box's exterior can be customised with CMYK digital print. It is supplied flat-packed for easy postage and storage, and its self-locking design ensures hassle-free assembly. The assembled internal dimensions are 262mm x 88mm x 335mm (LxWxH).
