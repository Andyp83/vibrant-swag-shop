Die Cut Box with Locking Lid - 175x130x65mm

PLU 124905

Custom gift box made from durable 1.5mm e-flute corrugated cardboard to provide reliable protection during transit. This box is perfect for gifting and building strong brand recognition. It is supplied flat-packed for easy postage and storage, and its self-locking design ensures hassle-free assembly. The assembled internal dimensions are 175mm x 130mm x 65mm (LxWxH).
