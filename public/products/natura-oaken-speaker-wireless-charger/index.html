NATURA Oaken Speaker Wireless Charger

PLU 121838

Stylish 5W Bluetooth speaker with built-in wireless charging capability. Oaken is manufactured from wheat straw fibre and ABS with a natural cork charging surface. It will charge wirelessly enabled devices simply by placing them on the natural cork non-slip pad and will work with most modern phones. The speaker will play music for up to five hours and can be recharged via an 80cm Type-C charging cable which is included. Oaken is presented in natural kraft gift box with NATURA branding.
