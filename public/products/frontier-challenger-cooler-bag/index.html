Frontier Challenger Cooler Bag

PLU 127917

A stylish 25L cooler bag designed for outdoor adventures. Made from two-tone water-resistant Tarpaulin fabric, it keeps contents cool, secure, and protected from the elements. The removable window on the lid allows for quick access without unzipping the entire bag, while the spacious interior easily fits a box of 24 x 330ml cans. The main compartment is insulated with PE foam encased in a water-resistant PEVA lining. Padded shoulder straps with a comfort cover provide hands-free convenience, and the front slip pocket offers a high-impact branding area. Presented with a Frontier branded hangtag.
