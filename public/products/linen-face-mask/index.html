Linen Face Mask

PLU 121996

Premium Linen Face Mask which can be reused by hand washing it in warm water. This mask features a dart stitched into the front panel to create more structure, keeping the mask's material away from the nose and mouth. It also has matching white ear loops with adjustable silicone toggles to ensure one size will fit most people. This mask provides protection with two layers of material manufactured from 90gsm linen. This face mask is manufactured for use in the home or for recreational or occupational use.
