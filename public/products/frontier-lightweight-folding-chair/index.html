Frontier Lightweight Folding Chair

PLU 129379

This compact folding chair is ideal for storing in the car or day pack to take to campsites, picnics and events. The Frontier Lightweight Folding Chair features magnetic shock-cord poles that almost assemble themselves, making set up fast and effortless. Made from 600D polyester with a water-resistant coating to prevent moisture retention, with dual mesh panels that increase ventilation, it is held together by a steel frame with a 120kg weight load. Presented with a Frontier branded hangtag.
