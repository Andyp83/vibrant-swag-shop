Swiss Peak Urban Windbreaker

PLU 126588

The urban unisex Swiss Peak windbreaker is the perfect choice for protection against the elements. Crafted from 75gsm ripstop fabric with a water-resistant rating of 5000, this windbreaker is both stylish and durable. It conveniently folds into its own bag for easy storage and transportation and features a reliable YKK coil zipper with Swiss Peak embossed pulls. Thoughtfully designed, it includes two large inside pockets for safeguarding personal items and two external mesh-lined pockets secured with the same premium YKK zippers and comfortable embossed pulls. An adjustable elastic toggle in the hem and hood ensures a precise fit. Available in eight sizes, the Swiss Peak windbreaker seamlessly combines practicality with style.
