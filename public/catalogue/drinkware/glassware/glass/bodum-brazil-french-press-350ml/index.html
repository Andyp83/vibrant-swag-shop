Bodum Brazil French Press 350ml

PLU 133814 · Glassware · Glass

An iconic 350ml French press coffee maker designed for individual use, delivering full flavour and aroma with every brew. The borosilicate glass carafe preserves the coffee's natural flavour, while the BPA-free plastic frame, handle and lid are lightweight and finished with Bodum branding at the base. The stainless steel plunger and filter system helps prevent grounds escaping during pouring. A more environmentally friendly brewing method requiring no paper filters or plastic capsules. Presented in a printed Bodum branded gift box.
