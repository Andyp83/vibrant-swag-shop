Bodum Brazil French Press 1L

PLU 133813 · Glassware · Glass

An iconic 1L French press coffee maker designed to deliver full flavour and aroma with every brew. The borosilicate glass carafe preserves the coffee's natural flavour while the BPA-free plastic frame, handle and lid are lightweight and finished with Bodum branding at the base. A stainless steel plunger and filter system helps prevent grounds escaping when pouring. A more environmentally friendly brewing method requiring no paper filters or plastic capsules. Presented in a printed Bodum branded gift box.
