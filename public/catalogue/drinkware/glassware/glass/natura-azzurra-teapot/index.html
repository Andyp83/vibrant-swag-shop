NATURA Azzurra Teapot

PLU 126536 · Glassware · Glass

This stylish wide-base teapot offers a 460ml capacity, perfect for one or two servings of your favourite tea. Its internal stainless steel infuser is designed to prevent even the finest tea leaves from escaping, ensuring a flawless hot brew every time. For easy maintenance, the infuser can be conveniently removed and washed. The teapot is complemented by a charming bamboo lid, securely fitted with a silicone seal to prevent it from falling when the pot is tipped. This product is presented in an individual natural gift box with NATURA branding.
