Luigi Bormioli Optica Flute

PLU 123296 · Glassware · Glass

Luigi Bormioli produce beautiful Italian crafted drinkware and the Optica range brings together the retro look from the 1920's with a new age style of glasses. The 210ml Optica Flute is designed and made in Italy from lead-free crystal glass. Created with Luigi Bormioli's proprietary SON.hyx to strengthen and keep glass brilliant and created with their proprietary Titanium Reinforced permanent treatment to increase stem strength and durability.
