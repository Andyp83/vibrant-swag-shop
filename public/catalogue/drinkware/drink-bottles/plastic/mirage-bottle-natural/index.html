Mirage Bottle - Natural

PLU 120268 · Drink Bottles · Plastic

Unique 700ml drink bottle which is manufactured from a 50/50 blend of natural wheat straw fibre and BPA-free polypropylene. Using it to make a reusable drink bottle reduces the amount of plastic and produces a trendy textured natural look. Mirage can be presented in an optional natural gift box or gift tube. This product is not dishwasher safe and handwashing is recommended. As this product is made using natural resources, the material may retain some odour which will fade over time.
