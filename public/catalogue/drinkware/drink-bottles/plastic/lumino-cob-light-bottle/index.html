Lumino COB Light Bottle

PLU 200262 · Drink Bottles · Plastic

Technology-driven, 700ml water bottle that is manufactured from crystal-clear Tritan which is BPA-free, shatterproof and odour-resistant. It has a unique screw on lid with twin light functions that use radical COB (Chips on Board) technology to increase light intensity and battery life. Lumino can be used as a powerful torch or a lantern and it is easily recharged from a laptop, a power bank or a USB mains adapter. Other features include push button operation and a strong woven carry loop which can be attached to a bag. It is presented in a black gift box with a Micro-B USB cable and an instruction leaflet. This product is not dishwasher safe and handwashing is recommended.
