Venus Bottle - Neoprene Sleeve

PLU 112544 · Drink Bottles · Glass

Deluxe 600ml borosilicate glass drink bottle which features an insulating soft neoprene sleeve that helps to keep drinks cool. The sleeve is nicely accented with grey neoprene trim. It has a secure screw on stainless steel lid which can be laser engraved with a logo. Borosilicate is a lightweight glass that is exceptionally strong and shatter-resistant. Glass is a natural choice for a clean, pure taste and is a safe and reusable plastic alternative. This product is not intended to be used with carbonated or hot liquids. It is not dishwasher safe and handwashing is recommended.
