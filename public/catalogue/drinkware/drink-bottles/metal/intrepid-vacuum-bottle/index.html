Intrepid Vacuum Bottle

PLU 120512 · Drink Bottles · Metal

600ml double wall, vacuum insulated stainless steel bottle with a stunning powder coated finish and will keep drinks cold for 24 hours or hot for up to 12 hours. Intrepid is BPA-free and features a secure screw on metal lid. The lid features a carry handle and carabiner so the bottle can easily transported and attached to a bag. Intrepid laser engraves to a shiny steel finish and is presented in a black gift box. This product is not dishwasher safe, and handwashing is recommended.
