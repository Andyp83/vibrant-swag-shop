SPICE Calypso Bottle 500ml - Full Colour

PLU 128807 · Drink Bottles · Metal

SPICE strive to create extraordinary products that have a positive impact on the planet, and their Calypso range is no different. This ergonomic 500ml drink bottle is 100% carbon neutral to delivery, made from recycled stainless steel with a sleek painted finish. It features TempLock double wall, vacuum insulation to maintain drink temperatures for longer, and has a large full colour branding area for maximum brand visibility. Calypso is 35% lighter than similar bottles on the market, and a secure, screw on lid ensures the bottle is leak-proof, perfect for day trips or commuting. The bottle is presented in a natural gift box with black print, featuring the SPICE logo on the back of the box. Production lead time is 40 working days on this product. Sampling requires additional lead time.
