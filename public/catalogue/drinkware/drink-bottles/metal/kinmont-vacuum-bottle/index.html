Kinmont Vacuum Bottle

PLU 126945 · Drink Bottles · Metal

This BPA-free 800ml robust drink bottle is ideal for staying hydrated on the go. Combining stainless steel and polypropylene for a stylish drinking accessory. This double wall, vacuum insulated bottle features a powder coat finish and a secure screw on lid, with a choice of either a flip valve sipper, or a screw on chug cap. The Kinmont drink bottle blends practicality and style, highlighted by its carry handle and eye-catching silver rivet accents. This product is not dishwasher safe, and handwashing is recommended.
