Compadre Vacuum Bottle

PLU 126879 · Drink Bottles · Metal

This 1L vacuum insulated stainless steel drink bottle is available in a range of trendy, powder coated colours with silver accents that resonate with human emotion. Known as an "emotional support water bottle" the Compadre features colours and an ergonomic design that promote mental well-being and self-care, reinforcing the idea that everyday items can enhance emotional and physical health. The threaded lid includes a built-in polypropylene straw, and the bottle is elegantly presented in a natural gift box.
