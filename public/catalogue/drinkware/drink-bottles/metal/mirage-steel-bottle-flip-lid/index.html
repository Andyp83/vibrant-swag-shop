Mirage Steel Bottle - Flip Lid

PLU 113808 · Drink Bottles · Metal

Trendy 700ml stainless steel drink bottle with a timeless design. It has a secure screw on lid which has a flip out drinking valve with a straw and a removable silicone mouth piece. The lid also has a convenient carry handle with a spring loaded carabiner clip. Mirage bottle laser engraves to a shiny steel finish, except for the silver bottle which engraves to a natural etch. Mirage is BPA-free and can be presented in an optional natural gift box. This product is not dishwasher safe and handwashing is recommended.
