Keepsake Vacuum Drink Bottle

PLU 127196 · Drink Bottles · Metal

Attention to detail is of the essence with this 580ml recycled stainless steel double wall vacuum bottle. Its unique shape is complemented by a natural acacia lid, which recesses beautifully into the bottleneck, exposing a designer-finished silver edging for an elegant touch. This Keepsake bottle is BPA-free and will keep drinks cold for 24 hours or hot for up to 12 hours. The stainless-steel handle ensures easy carrying, and the bottle comes presented in a Keepsake natural gift box, making it a sophisticated and functional accessory for anyone who appreciates a touch of class.
