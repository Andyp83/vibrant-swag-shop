Compostable Paper Coffee Cup - 180ml

PLU 129536 · Coffee Cups & Travel Mugs · Paper & Cardboard

Small 180ml (6oz) compostable paper coffee cup and lid. The cup features an aqueous coated lining to prevent leaks and maintain cup integrity and can be customised with full colour printing - perfect for showcasing your brand in cafes, at events, or even during daily commutes. Both the cup and lid can be composted and are suitable for hot and cold drinks. A production lead time of 17 working days applies to this product.
