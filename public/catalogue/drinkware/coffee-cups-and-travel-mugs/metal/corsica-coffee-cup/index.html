Corsica Coffee Cup

PLU 129665 · Coffee Cups & Travel Mugs · Metal

The Corsica Coffee Cup is a 350ml double wall coffee cup with a clean, modern profile designed for everyday use. It features a sturdy stainless steel outer with a polypropylene inner, providing durability alongside practical insulation. The flip closure lid folds all the way back for easy drinking while the smooth, painted finish is available in a range of great colours to suit your brand. The colour match lid and cup completes the look for a consistent, considered appearance. Presented in a natural gift box.
