Cordia Ceramic Vacuum Cup

PLU 124823 · Coffee Cups & Travel Mugs · Ceramic

Smart 300ml double wall, vacuum insulated stainless steel coffee cup with a timeless curved design and a stunning powder coated finish. It features a ceramic lining which provides a clean taste and additional insulation, and will keep drinks hot or cold for hours. Cordia laser engraves to a shiny steel finish and has a transparent push on lid with a sliding closure. The cup is BPA-free and is presented in a natural gift box.
