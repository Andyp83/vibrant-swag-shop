A4 Presentation Folder with Twin Pockets

PLU 120424 · Pads & Planners · Paper & Cardboard

Large capacity A4 presentation folder with twin pockets, a 6mm spine and a business card holder. It is manufactured from 300gsm gloss cardboard with matt cardboard available on request. The folder is supplied flat packed and is easily assembled in a few seconds. It can be printed edge-to-edge in stunning full colour on one or both sides and is finished with a clear aqueous coating to ensure the printing is scuff resistant. PMS colour matches are approximate. A lead time of 10 working days applies to this product.
