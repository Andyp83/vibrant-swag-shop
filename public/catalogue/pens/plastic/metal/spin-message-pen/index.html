Spin Message Pen

PLU 110819 · Plastic · Metal

Retractable plastic ballpoint pen with a soft rubber grip, metallic trim and a rotating inner barrel which holds six different messages. Each time the push button is clicked, a new message is displayed in the clear barrel window. It has a blue or black refill with 1200 metres of German manufactured Dokumental ink. A production lead time of 40 working days applies to this product by air freight and orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
