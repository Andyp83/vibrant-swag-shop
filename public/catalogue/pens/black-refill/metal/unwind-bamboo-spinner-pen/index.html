Unwind Bamboo Spinner Pen

PLU 129397 · Black Refill · Metal

Keep hands busy and minds engaged with this unique retractable ballpoint pen, featuring a built-in fidget spinner ring. The barrel and pusher are crafted from natural bamboo, complemented by recycled components throughout. The matte-finish spinner is available in a wide range of colours, while the pen writes smoothly with up to 1000m of Dokumental ink. Custom branding can be applied to both the spinner and barrel, and it is presented in a Kraft paper sleeve.
