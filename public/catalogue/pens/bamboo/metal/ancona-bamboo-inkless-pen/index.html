Ancona Bamboo Inkless Pen

PLU 122427 · Bamboo · Metal

The Ancona Bamboo Inkless Pen has a unique metal alloy tip that deposits small amounts of metal onto the page and has a writing distance of up to 5000m. Ancona has a natural bamboo barrel with shiny chrome trim and it can be presented in an optional gift box. With no sharpening or refills needed, this is an excellent environmentally conscious option.
