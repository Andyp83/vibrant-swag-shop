Infinity Inkless Bamboo Pen

PLU 129411 · Bamboo · Metal

The Infinity Inkless Bamboo Pen has a unique metal alloy tip that deposits small amounts of metal onto the page and has a writing distance of up to 5000m. With a bamboo barrel and kraft paper cap, as well as no sharpening or refills needed, this is an excellent environmentally conscious writing option.
