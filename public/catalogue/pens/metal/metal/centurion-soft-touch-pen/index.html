Centurion Soft-Touch Pen

PLU 129486 · Metal · Metal

The Centurion Soft-Touch Pen is a sleek, retractable ballpoint pen designed for everyday writing. Its recycled aluminium barrel features a soft-touch rubberised finish with shiny chrome trim for a premium feel. Equipped with a 1mm ball pen refill delivering 1200m of black Dokumental ink, it offers long-lasting performance. It can be presented in an optional cardboard pen sleeve at an additional cost.
