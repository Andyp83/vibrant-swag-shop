Eon Spinner Pen

PLU 129580 · Metal · Metal

A retractable recycled aluminium ballpoint pen combining everyday writing performance with an integrated fidget spinner ring. The smooth spinning action of the recycled aluminium ring adds a fun and tactile element during use, while the matte finish barrel with shiny chrome trim delivers a modern, refined appearance. It is fitted with a Parker-style refill containing 1800m of black Dokumental ink, providing long-lasting and consistent writing performance. An optional pen sleeve is available at additional cost.
