Montfort is a classic twist-action ballpoint pen with timeless French design. It has a brass barrel and a black large volume refill with 4000 metres of German manufactured Dokumental ink. Montfort has the Pierre Cardin logo tastefully engraved on the centre ring and can be presented in an optional Pierre Cardin sleeve or Pierre Cardin gift box.
