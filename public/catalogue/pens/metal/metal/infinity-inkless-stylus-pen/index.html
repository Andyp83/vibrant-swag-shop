Infinity Inkless Stylus Pen

PLU 121415 · Metal · Metal

The aluminium Infinity Inkless Pen has a unique metal alloy tip that deposits small amounts of metal onto the page and has a writing distance of up to 5000m. This pen features shiny chrome trim, a stylus and an eraser. With no sharpening or refills needed, this is an excellent environmentally conscious option. Infinity Inkless Stylus Pen can be presented in an optional pen sleeve.
