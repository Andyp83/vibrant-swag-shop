Packable Bucket Hat

PLU 126400 · Hats · Fabric & Textile

Compact and convenient foldaway polyester bucket hat. This lightweight bucket hat is quick to dry and easily folds away into a small pouch with a snap closure and carabiner for attaching to bags, making it the perfect accessory for hiking, camping and travel. The hat includes a removable chin strap with a toggle, and a toggle at the back of the hat to adjust the size. This handy hat is available in two sizes to fit most people.
