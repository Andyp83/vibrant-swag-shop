Cabana Wide Brim Hat

PLU 112787 · Hats · Metal

Popular wide brim sun hat which is made from cotton twill. The hat's material provides excellent UPF50 protection from the sun and complies with AS/NZS standards for sun protection. Cabana Wide Brim Hat is available in three sizes, including a child's size. It features a sweatband, smart gunmetal eyelets and a woven chin strap which has a breakaway safety clip and a sliding adjustment toggle.
