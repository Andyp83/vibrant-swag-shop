Kenai Custom Knitted Beanie

PLU 117533 · Beanies · Fabric & Textile

Fully customised jacquard knitted acrylic beanie with a folded cuff and a pompom. It can be knitted in up to four PMS colours at no extra cost and up to three of these colours can be used to make the pompom. PMS colour matching is approximate and one size will fit most people. Embroidery is available for an additional charge. A production lead time of 40 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
