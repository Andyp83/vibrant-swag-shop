Ezy Badge

PLU 108490 · Stationery · Paper & Cardboard

A unique double-sided adhesive badge incorporated into an A4 information sheet which can be printed on any laser printer or copier. After printing, simply peel and fold the badge then clip it onto a lanyard. Relevant information on the sheet can be retained by the user for reference. It is ideal for conferences, events, trade shows, site visitors, seminars, meetings, health and safety etc. Lanyards and badge holders are available on request. Please note this product is supplied unbranded and includes a branding template.
