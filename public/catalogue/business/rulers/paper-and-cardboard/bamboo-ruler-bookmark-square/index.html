Bamboo Ruler Bookmark - Square

PLU 121726 · Rulers · Paper & Cardboard

Bamboo ruler bookmark with a pad of yellow adhesive notes and five pads of different coloured adhesive flags. Each pad comes with 25 adhesive leaves. Measurements are printed with black ink in centimetres and inches. Bamboo is a natural material and because the plant is fast growing, it places less demands on natural resources and produces a material that is durable and versatile. Bamboo can also produce unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding.
