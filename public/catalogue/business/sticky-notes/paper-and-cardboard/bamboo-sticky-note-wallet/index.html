Bamboo Sticky Note Wallet

PLU 121728 · Sticky Notes · Paper & Cardboard

Bamboo side opening wiro bound wallet featuring 40 leaves (80 pages) of 70gsm paper, five different coloured pads of adhesive flags and has a pad of 25 adhesive notes. Bamboo is a natural material and because the plant is fast growing, it places less demands on natural resources and produces a material that is durable and versatile. Bamboo can also produce unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding.
