Pierre Cardin Valence Portfolio

PLU 120580 · Portfolios · Paper & Cardboard

Classically designed portfolio from Pierre Cardin which is hand crafted from the highest quality leather-look PU. It has a smart magnetic closure and a Pierre Cardin note pad with 30 leaves of 80gsm lined cream paper. Valence has a large slip pocket which will hold a tablet and a smaller zippered pocket. Other features include a card holder with a window, a phone pocket, four card pockets and an elastic pen loop. The Pierre Cardin logo is tastefully engraved on the magnetic closure and debossed on both the phone holder and the back of the portfolio. Valence is beautifully presented in a premium Pierre Cardin gift box.
