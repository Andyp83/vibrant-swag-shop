4-in-1 Mouse Mat

PLU 110542 · Desk Items · Fabric & Textile

Extremely thin silicone-based mouse mat with a precise microfibre mousing surface. It can be stored inside a laptop and doubles as a screen and keyboard protector as well as a handy screen cleaner. The mat can be washed and reused and the surface tension on the silicone base allows it to stick to most non-porous surfaces, providing a secure non-slip base which is fully repositionable. Instructions for use are included. A production lead time of 20 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
