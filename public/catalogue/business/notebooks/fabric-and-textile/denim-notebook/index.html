Denim Notebook

PLU 124869 · Notebooks · Fabric & Textile

This medium-sized (approximately A5) hard cover notebook has a stylish denim fabric finish and features a leather-look PU accent panel. An optional paper belly band is available on request and can be branded in full colour.
