Omega Notebook and Pen Gift Set

PLU 129682 · Notebooks · Metal

A coordinated notebook and pen set with a polished, professional finish. The medium-sized (approximately A5) Omega Notebook features a hard cover with a smooth PU finish, 80 leaves of 70gsm lined white paper, and a matching elastic closure and bookmark ribbon. It is paired with the Centurion Soft-Touch Pen, featuring a recycled aluminium barrel with a soft-touch rubberised finish and shiny chrome trim. With 1200 metres of black Dokumental ink, it delivers a smooth, reliable writing experience with a premium feel. An optional paper belly band is available on request and can be branded in full colour. The notebook and pen set is presented in a black gift box.
