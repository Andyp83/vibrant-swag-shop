Kingston Notebook and Pen Gift Set

PLU 129679 · Notebooks · Metal

A coordinated notebook and pen set with a sleek, professional look. The medium-sized (approximately A5) Kingston Notebook features a hard cover with a refined, premium PU finish, 80 leaves of 70gsm lined white paper, and a matching elastic closure band, bookmark ribbon, and pen loop. It is paired with the Panama Pen - Corporate, featuring a lacquered recycled aluminium barrel, shiny chrome trim, and 1200 metres of black Dokumental ink for smooth, reliable writing. An optional paper belly band is available on request and can be branded in full colour. The notebook and pen set is presented in a black gift box.
