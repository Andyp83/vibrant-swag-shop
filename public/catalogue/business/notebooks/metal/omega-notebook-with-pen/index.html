Omega Notebook With Pen

PLU 108827 · Notebooks · Metal

Smart medium-size (approximately A5) notebook with 80 lined 70gsm leaves (160 pages), a bookmark ribbon, an elastic closure band and a pen loop. It has a hard PU cover and is supplied with a matching Omega Pen. Please note that the pen should only be considered an approximate match. An optional paper belly band is available on request and can be branded in full colour.
