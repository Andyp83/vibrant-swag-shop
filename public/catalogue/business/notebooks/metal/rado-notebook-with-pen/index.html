Rado Notebook with Pen

PLU 110463 · Notebooks · Metal

Refined medium-size (approximately A5) notebook with 80 leaves (160 pages) of 80gsm lined cream paper, an elastic closure band and a unique pen holder on the inside of the spine. It has a hard cover with a textured PU finish and a stylish aluminium Lancer Pen is included in the pen holder. An optional paper belly band is available on request and can be branded in full colour.
