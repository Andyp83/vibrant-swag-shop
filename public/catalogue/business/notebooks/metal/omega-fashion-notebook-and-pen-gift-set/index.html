Omega Fashion Notebook and Pen Gift Set

PLU 129675 · Notebooks · Metal

A stylish, coordinated notebook and pen gift set available in a range of trendy fashion colours and presented in a natural gift box. The medium-sized (approximately A5) Omega Notebook features a durable PU hard cover, 80 leaves of lined white paper, and a matching elastic closure with bookmark ribbon. It is paired with an attractive Lancer Fashion Pen with a matte lacquered recycled aluminium barrel, matte silver trim, and 800 metres of black Dokumental ink for smooth, dependable writing. An optional paper belly band is available on request and can be branded in full colour. Colour matched and presented in a natural gift box.
