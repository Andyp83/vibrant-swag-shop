Omega Black Notebook

PLU 113892 · Notebooks · Paper & Cardboard

Smart medium-size (approximately A5) notebook with 80 lined 70gsm leaves (160 pages) and a black PU cover. It has colour edged pages to match the bookmark ribbon and the elastic closure band which are all matched as closely as the materials allow. An optional paper belly band is available on request and can be branded in full colour.
