Alexis Notebook

PLU 113597 · Notebooks · Paper & Cardboard

Medium-size (approximately A5) notebook with an attention-grabbing hard cover which is finished with a stylish combination of heather style PU matched with leather-look white PU. It has 80 leaves (160 pages) of 80gsm lined cream paper with a bookmark ribbon and an elastic closure band. An optional paper belly band is available on request and can be branded in full colour.
