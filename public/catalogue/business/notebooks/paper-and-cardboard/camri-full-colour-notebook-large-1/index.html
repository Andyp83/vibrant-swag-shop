Camri Full Colour Notebook - Large

PLU 118182 · Notebooks · Paper & Cardboard

Fully customised A4 size notebook with saddle stitch binding and 32 leaves (64 pages) of 70gsm cream paper which is available lined or unlined. Individual pages can be customised in one colour or full colour for an additional cost. The notebook has a 250gsm cardboard cover which can be branded with stunning edge-to-edge full colour print on the front and back. An optional paper belly band is available and can be branded in full colour. A production lead time of 35 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
