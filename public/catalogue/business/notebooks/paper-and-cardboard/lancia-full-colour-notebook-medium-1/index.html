Lancia Full Colour Notebook - Medium

PLU 118176 · Notebooks · Paper & Cardboard

Fully customised A5 size notebook with silver wiro binding and 60 leaves (120 pages) of 70gsm cream paper which is available lined or unlined. Individual pages can be customised in one colour or full colour for an additional cost. The notebook has an 1100gsm unbleached grey cardboard back cover and a 350gsm cardboard front cover which can be branded with stunning edge-to-edge full colour print. A production lead time of 35 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
