Meridian Notebook

PLU 111461 · Notebooks · Paper & Cardboard

Exceptional medium-size (approximately A5) notebook with 96 leaves (192 pages) of 80gsm lined cream paper. It has a hard cover that is finished with textured, heather style PU which gives it a trendy woven look. Other features include colour-edged pages, a bookmark ribbon, an elastic closure band and an elastic pen loop on the spine. Meridian thermo debosses to a stunning two-tone finish.
