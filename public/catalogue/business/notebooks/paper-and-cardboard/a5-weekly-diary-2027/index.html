A5 Weekly Diary - 2027

PLU 122388 · Notebooks · Paper & Cardboard

This A5 weekly diary is dated from January to December and is formatted to show a week per double spread. It features 80 leaves (160pages) of ivory coloured 80gsm paper with round edges and a textured leather look PU cover. The diary has a contact page, a yearly planner, public holidays, self-care goals and achievements, trips/events, project planning and space for notes. Other features include an elastic closure band, bookmark ribbon, and a document pocket.
