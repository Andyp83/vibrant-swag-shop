Supra Full Colour Notebook

PLU 118183 · Notebooks · Paper & Cardboard

Fully customised, medium-size (approximately A5) notebook with a hard cover and 64 leaves (128 pages) of 70gsm cream paper which is available lined or unlined. Individual pages can be customised in one colour or full colour for an additional cost. The cover can be branded on both sides with stunning edge-to-edge full colour print. Supra has a bookmark ribbon and an elastic closure band which can be mixed and matched from the standard colours. An optional paper belly band is available on request and can be branded in full colour. A production lead time of 45 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
