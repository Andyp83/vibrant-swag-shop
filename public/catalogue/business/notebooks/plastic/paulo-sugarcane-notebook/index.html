Paulo Sugarcane Notebook

PLU 127792 · Notebooks · Plastic

A practical and elegant medium-sized (approximately A5) hardcover notebook made with 80% sugarcane waste. It includes 80 leaves (160 pages) of smooth 90gsm natural lined paper, making it ideal for everyday use. The hard cover is enhanced with an RPET trim in a striking contrasting colour. Perfect for professionals, students, and creatives seeking quality and thoughtful design.
