Kraft Notebook and Pen Gift Set

A coordinated notebook and pen set with a clean, natural look for everyday organisation. It is paired with a retractable Lancer Kraft Pen with a natural or black kraft paper barrel, shiny chrome trim, and 1200 metres of black Dokumental ink for smooth, reliable writing. The notebook and pen set is presented in a natural gift box.
