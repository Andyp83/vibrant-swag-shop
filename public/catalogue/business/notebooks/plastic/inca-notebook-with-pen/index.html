Inca Notebook with Pen

PLU 116303 · Notebooks · Plastic

Medium-size (approximately A5) notebook with a hard cover which is finished with a stylish combination of on trend natural cork and smooth PU. It has 80 leaves (160 pages) of 70gsm lined cream paper, an elastic closure band and an elastic pen loop which holds a matching retractable ball pen with a cork barrel. The pen has a black refill with 1000 metres of German manufactured Dokumental ink and a tungsten carbide ball for improved writing quality.
