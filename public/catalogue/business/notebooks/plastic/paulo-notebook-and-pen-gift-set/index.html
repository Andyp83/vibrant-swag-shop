Paulo Notebook and Pen Gift Set

PLU 129677 · Notebooks · Plastic

A coordinated notebook and pen set with a distinctive, contemporary look. The medium-sized (approximately A5) notebook features a hard cover with contrasting RPET trim and 80 leaves of lined natural paper, both made from 80% sugarcane waste. It is paired with the Savannah Recycled Plastic Pen, featuring a matte finished recycled ABS barrel, a natural bamboo pusher and 1000 metres of black Dokumental ink for smooth, reliable writing. The notebook and pen set is presented in a natural gift box.
