Wine Carry Pack - Double

PLU 126068 · Gift Boxes · Paper & Cardboard

Custom wine presentation box made from durable 1.5mm e-flute corrugated cardboard with display windows. This box holds two standard wine bottles, perfect for gifting and building strong brand recognition. Featuring a convenient carry handle, the box's exterior can be customised with CMYK digital print. It is supplied flat-packed for easy postage and storage, and its self-locking design ensures hassle-free assembly. The assembled dimensions are 177mm x 88mm x 335mm (LxWxH).
