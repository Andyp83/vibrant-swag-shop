Personalised Gift Wrapping Paper

PLU 120908 · Packaging Accessories · Paper & Cardboard

Quality 60gsm gloss custom gift wrapping paper supplied in 700mm x 500mm sheets, folded down to 250x175mm. Edge-to-edge printing is available in up to four spot colours. When the paper is trimmed during production, it is unavoidable that some of the artwork will be randomly cropped off. A production lead time of 40 working days applies to this product by air freight, and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
