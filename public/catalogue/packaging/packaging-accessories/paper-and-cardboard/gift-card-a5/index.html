Gift Card - A5

PLU 121193 · Packaging Accessories · Paper & Cardboard

Folding cards which are ideal for a range of events including Christmas, birthdays, congratulations, gifting and greeting etc. The cards are printed on 300gsm gloss cardboard and supplied flat with a crease, so they are ready to fold down to A5 size. Matte finish cardboard is available on request. They can be branded on both sides with edge-to-edge printing in full colour or closely matched spot colours. A production lead time of 15 working days applies to this product.
