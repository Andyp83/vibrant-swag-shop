Silicone Wrist Band - Glow in the Dark

PLU 112807 · Wristbands · Silicone

Wrist bands which are manufactured from a special silicone that glows in the dark. They are ideal for night clubs, beach parties, concerts and any night time event. The wrist bands can be branded with the choice of screen printing, debossing or embossing and one size fits most. They can be supplied in an optional polybag with or without a full colour label. A production lead time of 20 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
