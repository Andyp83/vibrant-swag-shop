Silicone Wrist Band - Embossed

PLU 112806 · Wristbands · Silicone

Silicone wrist bands have many uses and are ideal for fundraising, awareness campaigns, promotion and as admission bracelets for events. There are 14 standard colours and PMS colours are available on request. They are branded with embossing which raises the logo above the surface. All or part of the raised surface can be printed with one colour which is optional. One size fits all and they can be supplied in an optional polybag, with or without a full colour label. A production lead time of 20 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
