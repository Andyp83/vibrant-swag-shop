Bottle Buddy

PLU 109758 · Stubby & Can Holders · Fabric & Textile

Zip up wetsuit style stubby holder that will fit most stubby size bottles. It is manufactured from 4mm laminated open cell foam material which has powerful insulation properties to keep drinks cool as well as preventing cold wet hands when holding chilled bottles. Bottle Buddy is supplied flat packed for economical transportation and storage.
