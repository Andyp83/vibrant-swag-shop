Mini Tumbling Tower

PLU 121506 · Games & Puzzles · Fabric & Textile

Tumbling Tower is a game of physical skill where a tower of 48 wooden blocks is constructed, and players take turns removing one block at a time without causing the tower to fall. The game is beautifully presented in a drawstring bag and comes with instructions. Wood is a natural material which produces unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding.
