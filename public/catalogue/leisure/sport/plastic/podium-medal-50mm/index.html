Podium Medal - 50mm

PLU 115696 · Sport · Plastic

50mm diameter medal which is available in gold, silver and bronze. It can be ordered in one colour or a combination of colours at no extra cost. The medals are branded with the choice of laser engraving or a full colour resin coated finish on the front and laser engraving on the back. Each medal is supplied with a ribbon lanyard which has a safety clip and can be branded in one colour on one side. The medals laser engrave to a natural etch.
