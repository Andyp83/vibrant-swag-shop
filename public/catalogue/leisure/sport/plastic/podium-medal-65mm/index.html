Podium Medal - 65mm

PLU 115692 · Sport · Plastic

65mm diameter medal which is available in gold, silver and bronze. It can be ordered in one colour or a combination of colours at no extra cost. The medals are branded with the choice of laser engraving or a full colour resin coated finish on the front and laser engraving on the back. Each medal is supplied with a ribbon lanyard which has a safety clip and can be branded in one colour on one side. The medals laser engrave to a natural etch.
