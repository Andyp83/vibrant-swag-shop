Carabiner Tube

PLU 111936 · Sport · Plastic

Small presentation container with a screw on lid and a spring-loaded carabiner clip which is ideal for presenting a Yeti Premium Cooling Towel. The clip can be attached to a bag and when the tube is stored inside a bag it protects the contents when the towel is wet. It can also hold a polishing cloth and other small products like ear buds and confectionery etc.
