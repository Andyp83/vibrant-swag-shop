Active Cooling Towel - Pouch

PLU 112971 · Sport · Fabric & Textile

Polyester cooling towel which can also be used as a handy sports or gym towel. It will cool up to 15 degrees below the outdoor temperature in seconds when wet. Simply wet, wring out and snap tight to remove the excess water. Cooling will last as long as the towel remains wet and it is a natural, chemical free reaction. The fabric is constructed to maximise the cooling power of rapid evaporation and the towel is worn around the neck or head to stay cooler for longer. Active is ideal for use when playing sports, jogging, walking, biking, at the gym, working outdoors etc. It is presented in a re-sealable storage pouch or an optional carabineer tube, both of which include full instructions.
