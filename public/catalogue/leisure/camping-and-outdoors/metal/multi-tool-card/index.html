Multi-Tool Card

PLU 116111 · Camping & Outdoors · Metal

Stainless steel multi-tool that is credit card size and slim enough to fit in a wallet. It features a can opener, a knife edge, a screwdriver, a ruler, a two-position wrench, a four-position wrench, a butterfly wrench, a saw blade and a direction ancillary indicator for navigation. Multi-Tool Card can be used anywhere and is ideal for outdoor activities. It laser engraves to a stainless steel finish and is presented in a compact PU sleeve.
