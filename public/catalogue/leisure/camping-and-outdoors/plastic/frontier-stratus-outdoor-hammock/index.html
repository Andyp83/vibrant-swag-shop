Frontier Stratus Outdoor Hammock

PLU 129106 · Camping & Outdoors · Plastic

Lightweight, durable, and comfortable, the Frontier Stratus Outdoor Hammock is the perfect companion for your next adventure. Made from strong 210T nylon, it fits two people with ease and packs down into an integrated pouch for simple storage. The provided straps and carabiners have been designed not to damage the trees it's attached to. With five different hanging options this hammock is perfect for camping trips, hiking trails, or relaxing in your backyard. Presented with a Frontier branded hangtag.
