Aurora COB Lantern

PLU 112193 · Camping & Outdoors · Plastic

Extremely bright lantern that is powered by three super bright light modules. Aurora uses radical COB (Chip-on-Board) technology which increases both the light intensity and the battery life. Simply pull the lantern open to operate and close to turn the light off. It has carry handles which fold away when not in use and it uses three AA batteries which are included.
