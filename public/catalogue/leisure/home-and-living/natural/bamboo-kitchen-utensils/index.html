Bamboo Kitchen Utensils

PLU 121522 · Home & Living · Natural

Stylish Bamboo Kitchen Utensils set which includes a spoon, spatula, fork, and a spoon with a hole designed to conveniently measure a spaghetti serving per person. The utensils comes with a handy bamboo box which will help store the items all together so that they are within reach on your kitchen bench when you need them. The Bamboo Kitchen Utensil set comes presented in a natural gift box. Bamboo is a natural material and because the plant is fast growing, it places less demands on natural resources and produces a material that is durable and versatile. Bamboo can also produce unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding.
