Montgomery Cheese Board

PLU 115957 · Home & Living · Natural

Elegant cheese board which is manufactured from acacia wood. It has four soft non-marking pads on the base and a compact drawer which opens to reveal a set of three cheese knives and a cheese fork. Montgomery laser engraves to a natural etch and is beautifully presented in a matt black gift box. Wood is a natural material which produces unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding.
