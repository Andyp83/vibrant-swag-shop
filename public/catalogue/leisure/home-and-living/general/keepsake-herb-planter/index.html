Keepsake Herb Planter

PLU 126961 · Home & Living · General

Brighten up your windowsill with this stylish herb planter, perfect for growing your favourite herbs. Made from iron and finished in an elegant gloss white, each set includes slate markers and chalk for easy labelling, so you can organise and keep track of what you are growing. A drip tray is also included to protect surfaces from water damage. Whether you are a seasoned gardener or just starting out, these pots make herb cultivation simple, bringing a touch of nature into your home. Presented in a beautiful Keepsake gift box, this set makes a thoughtful and stylish gift.
