Keepsake Halo Lantern

PLU 126892 · Home & Living · Fabric & Textile

A gift to fall in love with, this compact Manhattan loft style lamp will add a touch of class to any interior. With its vintage design and adjustable brightness, the warm, inviting light creates a nostalgic ambience, enhancing both the aesthetic and comfort of your home. Whether you want a soft, dim glow for a relaxing evening or a brighter light for reading at a desk, the Halo Lantern offers versatile lighting options to suit your needs. The rechargeable lantern can run for up to 15 hours before needing to be recharged, making it convenient to move around the home and use wherever you need it. Presented in a beautiful keepsake gift box, this lantern makes a thoughtful and beautiful gift. It is presented in a natural gift box with a Keepsake branded sleeve.
