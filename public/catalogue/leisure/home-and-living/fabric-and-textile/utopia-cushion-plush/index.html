Utopia Cushion - Plush

PLU 123626 · Home & Living · Fabric & Textile

Comfortable 40cm x 40cm cushion which has a pleasant soft plush finish. It can be branded on both sides with stunning edge to edge full colour graphics. Each side of the cushion can have a different design and there is an additional setup charge for this. The outer cover is manufactured from robust poly-plush material and it has an invisible zippered closure so it is easily removed for washing. The inner cushion has a cotton cover and is filled with a down like cotton filling. Each Utopia cushion is presented in a unique flat-pack vacuum sleeve which removes the air from inside the product. This significantly reduces the size to allow for cost effective shipping and easy individual distribution. A production lead time of 35 working days applies to this product.
