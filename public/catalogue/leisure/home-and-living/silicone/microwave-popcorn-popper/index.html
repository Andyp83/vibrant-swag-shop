Microwave Popcorn Popper

PLU 120339 · Home & Living · Silicone

Create delicious popcorn at home with this reusable popcorn popper that collapses flat for compact storage when not in use. Simply extend the popper into a bowl, add popcorn kernels, salt and oil, put the lid on, and microwave for 2-3 minutes to create fresh popcorn for movie nights and parties. Manufactured from BPA-free food-grade silicone, and the popper is presented in a white gift box with instructions for use.
