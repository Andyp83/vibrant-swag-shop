Bambino Lunch Box

PLU 118594 · Home & Living · Plastic

Eco-inspired lunch box which is manufactured from a unique 50/50 blend of natural wheat straw fibre and BPA-free polypropylene. The lunch box has a bamboo push on lid that is held securely in place with a silicone band and is presented in an unbleached cardboard gift box. Bamboo is a natural material that produces unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding. This product is not dishwasher safe and handwashing is recommended.
