Bamboo Cutlery Set

PLU 117633 · Home & Living · Plastic

Natural bamboo cutlery set which is presented in a cotton drawstring bag. The set includes a knife, fork, spoon, straw and a cleaning brush. This cutlery set is an environmentally-conscious alternative to single-use plastic cutlery and is a great way to send a positive message. Branding is on the cotton drawstring bag. Bamboo is a natural material and because the plant is fast growing, it places less demands on natural resources and produces a material that is durable and versatile. Bamboo can also produce unavoidable variances in the grain pattern and colour. This item is not dishwasher safe, and handwashing is recommended.
