Silicone Reusable Can Lid

PLU 118121 · Home & Living · Plastic

Keep opened cans of food fresh with a reusable silicone can lid. The durable BPA-free silicone lid provides a large branding area, will fit three different sizes of tin cans (67mm, 75mm and 85mm diameter) and has a convenient silicone tab for easy opening. This product provides a reusable alternative to single-use food coverings such as plastic wrap. A production lead time of 30 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
