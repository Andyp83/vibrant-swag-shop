Luncheon Expandable Lunch Box

PLU 129234 · Home & Living · Plastic

The Luncheon Expandable Lunch Box offers a smart design that adapts to different meal habits. Organising lunch is easy with two compartments which provide a 2L capacity that pack down to one offering 1.2L capacity and a cutlery set that fits inside the box. Secure side clips keep food protected on the move, while the black container and bamboo lid contrast adds a polished finish to this practical everyday essential.
