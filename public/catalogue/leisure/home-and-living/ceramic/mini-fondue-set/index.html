Mini Fondue Set

PLU 126524 · Home & Living · Ceramic

Mini ceramic fondue set that includes two stainless steel serving skewers with polypropylene handles. Simply melt cheese or chocolate in the fondue bowl using a tealight candle for heat, and dip various treats into the bowl using the skewers. This novel fondue set is sure to provide engaging entertainment for guests at dinners or parties. The set includes a tealight candle holder, a bowl and two skewers. Tealight candles are not included.
