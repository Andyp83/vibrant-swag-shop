Planter with Bamboo Base

PLU 120901 · Home & Living · Ceramic

Modern ceramic planter pot with bamboo base and legs for extra height which is suitable for indoor plants. The pot does not have a drainage hole. It is presented in an individual kraft box. Bamboo is a natural material and because the plant is fast growing, it places less demands on natural resources and produces a material that is durable and versatile. Bamboo can also produce unavoidable variances in the grain pattern and colour.
