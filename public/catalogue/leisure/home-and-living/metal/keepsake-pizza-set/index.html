Keepsake Pizza Set

PLU 126895 · Home & Living · Metal

Elevate your dining experience with this beautifully crafted acacia wooden pizza board and blade set, featuring a satin finish for an added touch of style and sophistication. The generously sized 46x30cm board provides ample space to present a large pizza to your guests, while the mitred front edge ensures effortless transfer of pizzas on/off the board. The accompanying Acacia wood and stainless steel curved blade not only complements the set but also slices through even the thickest pizzas with ease. Add a touch of sophistication to your table with this stylish pizza board and blade set.
