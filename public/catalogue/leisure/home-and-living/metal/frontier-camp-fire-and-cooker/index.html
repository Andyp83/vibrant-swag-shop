Frontier Camp Fire and Cooker

PLU 126565 · Home & Living · Metal

Satisfy your appetite for adventure with the Frontier Camp Fire Cooker. Made from stainless steel with a tough powder coating, this versatile cooker is ideal for camping, or using as a small cooker or fire in the garden. The cooker is fuelled by wood pellets, which are placed into the inner chamber. Once the fuel is alight, put the BBQ grill on top of the cooker and start grilling. The cooker is presented in natural kraft packaging with Frontier branding and includes a small bag of wood pellets and instructions for use.
