This clear acrylic fridge planner is designed as a versatile, reusable planning surface that adapts to any routine. Strong corner magnets keep it firmly in place on magnetic surfaces, while optional adhesive backing allows for flexible mounting. With a dry erase marker and clearly labelled components, it offers everything needed to organise busy kitchens or shared spaces.
