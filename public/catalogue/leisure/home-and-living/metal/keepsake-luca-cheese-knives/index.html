Keepsake Luca Cheese Knives

PLU 133410 · Home & Living · Metal

The Keepsake Luca Cheese Knives are three specialised knives, each designed for a different cheese. A perforated blade is perfect for soft varieties, while a straight slicer delivers clean, precise cuts through semi-soft varieties and the fork-tip knife makes serving effortless. Stainless steel blades paired with long, premium acacia handles give each knife a balanced, comfortable feel in hand. A thoughtful gift for entertainers and foodies alike. Presented in a natural kraft box with a Keepsake branded sleeve.
