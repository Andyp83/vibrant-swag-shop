Niagara Folding Chair

PLU 117602 · Chairs · Metal

Robust folding chair with comfortable arm rests, two mesh beverage holders and a matching carry bag. It is ideal for camping, the beach, picnics, barbecues and events. Niagara is manufactured from 600D polyester with a plastic water-resistant coating that prevents the fabric from retaining moisture when wet. It has a strong powder coated steel frame with a recommended static weight loading of up to 150kgs.
