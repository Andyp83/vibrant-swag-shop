SPICE Waste2Gear Umbrella

PLU 129689 · Umbrellas · Glass

This classy umbrella is made from recycled ocean nylon and features a 59cm eight panel canopy designed for reliable wet weather protection. Fibreglass ribs and shaft provide strength and flexibility, while rounded ABS tips enhance safety and durability. The cork handle adds a natural, comfortable grip and creates a strong visual contrast against the canopy for a distinctive, premium finish. It is 100% carbon neutral to delivery.
