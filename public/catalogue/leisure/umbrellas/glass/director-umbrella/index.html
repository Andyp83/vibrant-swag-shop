Director Umbrella

PLU 120307 · Umbrellas · Glass

The tri-folding Director Umbrella sports a generous canopy while folding down to a compact 33cm. It has an eight-panel vented pongee canopy with 57cm fibreglass ribs and a steel shaft. Other features include automatic opening and closing, a woven carry handle and a matching pongee drawstring sleeve.
