Hurricane City Umbrella

PLU 200581 · Umbrellas · Glass

The tri-folding City Umbrella is part of the premium Hurricane range, offering a generous canopy while collapsing to a compact 30cm. The eight-panel pongee canopy is vented and has a matching sleeve with Hurricane branding. City has automatic opening and closing, 57cm fibreglass ribs, a steel shaft and a rubberised hand grip.
