RPET Compact Umbrella

PLU 118215 · Umbrellas · Glass

Compact 54cm, triple-folding umbrella with an eight-rib frame and automatic opening and closing. The eight-panel canopy is manufactured from 190T RPET (recycled PET) pongee, produced by recycling used water bottles and food packaging. It has 54cm fibreglass ribs and a steel shaft and folds down to a compact 28cm. It has a matching pongee sleeve and a Japanese oak wooden handle.
