Bobby Bizz Anti-theft Backpack Briefcase

PLU 125282 · Backpacks · Metal

Exceptional 10L anti-theft backpack from XD that doubles as a secure briefcase, keeping belongings safe on daily commutes. Made from high-quality cut-resistant 900D polyester with PU coating and a reflective print, Bizz has two compartments padded with anti-shock foam that will fit a laptop up to 15.6" and a tablet up to 10". Easily attach a power bank to the built-in USB charging port to charge devices on the go. A built-in metal frame helps to protect items and ensure the bag retains its shape on any adventure. With a comfortable carry handle and padded shoulder straps that can be removed and stowed away, this versatile bag features an integrated zinc alloy lock, making it the perfect accessory for anyone on the go.
