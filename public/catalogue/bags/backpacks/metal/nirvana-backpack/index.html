Nirvana Backpack

PLU 113394 · Backpacks · Metal

Nirvana is where fashion meets function and this modern, on trend backpack has all the hallmarks of impeccable style and superior quality. It is manufactured from poly-canvas with an on-trend heather-style finish. Nirvana features the extra protection of a padded fold-over flap which has a magnetic closure with adjustable straps. The closure, buckles and rivets are all finished in shiny electroplated gunmetal. The bag has a heavily padded main compartment with a laptop sleeve, a padded outer compartment for a tablet and padded adjustable shoulder straps. Other features include a zippered external pocket on the front, two external side pockets and smart electroplated silver zips.
