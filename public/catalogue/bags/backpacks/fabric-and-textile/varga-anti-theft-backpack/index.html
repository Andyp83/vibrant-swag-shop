Varga Anti-Theft Backpack

PLU 116952 · Backpacks · Fabric & Textile

Security backpack which has a zippered rear pocket that cannot be opened while it is being worn and a main compartment with a hidden zipper. It is manufactured from on-trend heather-style 300D polyester with a 600D polyester base and 210D polyester lining. Varga has an internal padded laptop sleeve with an adjustable Velcro closure and two internal slip pockets. It has adjustable shoulder straps and breathable padding on the back and shoulders for extra comfort. Other features include a padded carry handle and a rear luggage strap so it can slide onto the handle of a rolling suitcase.
