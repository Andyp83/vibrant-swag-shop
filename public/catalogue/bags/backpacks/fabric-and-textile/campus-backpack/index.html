Campus Backpack

PLU 107675 · Backpacks · Fabric & Textile

Modern style backpack which is manufactured from rugged 600D polyester with a decorative patterned ripstop nylon front panel. It has an inner compartment to hold a laptop or a tablet, a padded back for comfortable carrying and a padded base. Other features include padded woven shoulder straps which are adjustable, a woven carry handle, a vertical zippered pocket on the front, a mesh water bottle holder and twin external straps for holding a jacket etc.
