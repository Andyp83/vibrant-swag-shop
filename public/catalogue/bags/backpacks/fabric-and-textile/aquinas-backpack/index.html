Aquinas Backpack

PLU 121425 · Backpacks · Fabric & Textile

Modern backpack which is manufactured from polycanvas with a water-resistant PU coating. It has an inner compartment to hold a laptop or a tablet, a padded back fitted with a zippered pocket for comfortable carrying and a padded base. Other features include padded woven shoulder straps which are adjustable, a woven carry handle, a large, zippered pocket on the front, and a water bottle holder.
