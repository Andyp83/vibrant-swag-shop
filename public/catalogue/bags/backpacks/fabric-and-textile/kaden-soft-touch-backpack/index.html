Kaden Soft-Touch Backpack

PLU 127716 · Backpacks · Fabric & Textile

Sleek and versatile, this slim design backpack expands effortlessly from 6L to 15L with a simple zip. Built for everyday adventures, the Kaden is made from reverse 600D polyester with water-resistant soft-touch PU coating. It features a spacious front compartment plus a padded inner sleeve that securely holds devices up to 16". The padded shoulder straps allow you to carry in comfort, while the luggage trolley strap adds convenience.
