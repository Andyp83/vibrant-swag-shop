Alumni Soft-Touch Backpack

PLU 127717 · Backpacks · Fabric & Textile

The Alumni is a practical, everyday backpack. Made from durable 600D polyester with a soft-touch PU coating, it features a padded internal device sleeve that securely fits laptops up to 16". In addition to the main compartment there are two external zippered pockets to help organise your belongings. The adjustable air-mesh straps are padded for comfort and the woven luggage trolley strap adds convenience when travelling.
