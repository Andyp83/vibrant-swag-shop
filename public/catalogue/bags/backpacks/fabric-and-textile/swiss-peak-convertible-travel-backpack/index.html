Swiss Peak Convertible Travel Backpack

PLU 125285 · Backpacks · Fabric & Textile

Swiss Peak offers a high-quality travel bag that can be converted quickly between a backpack, shoulder bag, cross-body, and hand-held bag. The bag is made from durable 600D and 1680D polyester and features a spacious 21L main compartment with a dedicated sleeve for a 15.6" laptop. The front pocket is ideal for pens and tech accessories, and features RIFD protected pockets. The shoulder straps are fully adjustable and removable for convenience, and a trolley luggage strap is included. The Swiss Peak logo is tastefully placed on the front of the bag.
