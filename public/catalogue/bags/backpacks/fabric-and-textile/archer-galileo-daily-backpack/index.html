ARCHER Galileo Daily Backpack

PLU 128642 · Backpacks · Fabric & Textile

Designed for life in motion, the ARCHER Galileo Daily Backpack combines clean, modern styling with practical versatility. Made from durable polyester with a subtle heathered finish, it features a spacious 19L main zippered compartment, plus a separate padded back section to protect your devices. Two internal zippered compartments and handy organising pockets keep essentials in order. Ergonomic straps, a padded back panel, and a luggage strap ensure everyday comfort and convenience. Finished with colour-match zips, ARCHER branded lining, and presented with an ARCHER branded hangtag.
