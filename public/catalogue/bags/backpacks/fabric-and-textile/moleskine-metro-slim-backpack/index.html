Moleskine Metro Slim Backpack

PLU 120902 · Backpacks · Fabric & Textile

The Moleskine Metro Slim Backpack is an urban lightweight backpack designed to go with you everywhere and carry the day's essentials. This luxury backpack features a flap with magnetic closure and a water-resistant zip to keep all your essentials safe. Inside, the backpack features an organizer, two pen holders, a business card holder, a secure pocket and an inner compartment to protect your laptop. The Metro Slim is fitted with an adjustable padded shoulder strap and breathable air-mesh back for exceptional comfort on the go and a rear luggage sleeve will slide over the handle of a rolling suitcase. A reflective Moleskine logo is sewn onto the leather-look PU base of the backpack. Please note this product cannot be supplied without the Moleskine logo. Moleskine reserves the right to decline any custom project or artwork that does not align with its brand on a case-by-case basis.
