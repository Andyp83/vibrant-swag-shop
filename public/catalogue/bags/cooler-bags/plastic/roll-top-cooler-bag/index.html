Roll Top Cooler Bag

PLU 120143 · Cooler Bags · Plastic

Sophisticated 14L cooler bag which is manufactured from smart 600D polyester with PE foam insulation encased in a water-resistant PEVA inner liner. The bag has a zippered roll top closure and secure clips to lock the ends away neatly. Other features include a gusset for increased capacity, a zippered external pocket and woven carry handles.
