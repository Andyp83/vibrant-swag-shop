Nirvana Wine Cooler Bag

PLU 116539 · Cooler Bags · Plastic

Nirvana is where fashion meets function and this modern wine cooler bag has all the hallmarks of impeccable style and superior quality. It has thick PE foam insulation encased in a water-resistant PEVA inner liner. The outer construction is poly-canvas with an on-trend heather style finish. Other features include an adjustable shoulder strap, a carry handle, a slip pocket on the front and a top closure with a smart electroplated silver zip.
