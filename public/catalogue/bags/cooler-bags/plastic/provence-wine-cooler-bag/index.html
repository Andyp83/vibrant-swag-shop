Provence Wine Cooler Bag

PLU 119419 · Cooler Bags · Plastic

Premium two-bottle wine carry bag which keeps wine cool while in transit. It is manufactured from 600D polyester with PE foam insulation encased in a water-resistant PEVA inner liner. Other features include strong woven carry handles, a zippered front pocket and a zippered main compartment with dome closures to lock the ends away neatly.
