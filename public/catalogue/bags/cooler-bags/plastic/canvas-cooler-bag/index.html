Canvas Cooler Bag

PLU 116660 · Cooler Bags · Plastic

Impressive 32L cooler bag with PE foam insulation encased in a PEVA inner liner. The outer construction is natural 410gsm heavy cotton canvas with stunning leather-look PU accents. It has woven carry handles with a domed cuff closure and woven shoulder straps which are adjustable and removable. Other features include a zippered top closure and a large front slip pocket.
