Zest Lunch Cooler Bag - Full Colour

PLU 117125 · Cooler Bags · Plastic

Smart 5L lunch cooler bag which is manufactured from tough 600D polyester. The outer panels can be branded with stunning edge-to-edge full colour print and it has PE foam insulation encased in a water-resistant PEVA inner liner. It has a black zippered top closure and a woven carry handle. The carry handle and the small panel behind the zip are available in the choice of 10 standard colours which can be mixed and matched. A production lead time of 30 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
