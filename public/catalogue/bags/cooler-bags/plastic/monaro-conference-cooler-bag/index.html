Monaro Conference Cooler Bag

PLU 113113 · Cooler Bags · Plastic

Unique satchel-style conference or event bag that is also a 15L cooler bag. This radical multi-purpose bag has increased sustainability as it can be used for many years after the event. Monaro has PE foam insulation encased in a water-resistant liner and the outer construction is rugged 600D polyester with a large zippered pocket on the front. Other features include a zippered top closure, an adjustable woven shoulder strap, trendy patterned piping on the front and a business card holder on the reverse.
