Aspiring Cooler Bag

PLU 115252 · Cooler Bags · Plastic

Impressive 13L cooler bag with thick PE foam insulation encased in a water-resistant PEVA inner liner. The outer construction is 600D polyester with a large zippered front pocket and two mesh side pockets. Other features include a zippered top closure, smart zipper pulls, a woven carry handle and trendy elastic lacing which is ideal for holding a sweater or light jacket.
