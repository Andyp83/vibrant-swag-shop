Vermont Cooler Seat

PLU 129450 · Cooler Bags · Metal

The Vermont Cooler Seat is the perfect companion for outdoor adventures. Its durable PVC coated 600D polyester construction, with PEVA lining and PE foam insulation keeps drinks and snacks cool while providing a comfortable seat. The steel frame supports a maximum static load weight of 80kg, while the lightweight, foldable design, removable cooler bag and shoulder strap make it easy to carry, store, and take anywhere.
