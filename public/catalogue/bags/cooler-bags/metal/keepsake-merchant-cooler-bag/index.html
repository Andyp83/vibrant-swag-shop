Keepsake Merchant Cooler Bag

PLU 126974 · Cooler Bags · Metal

This classically designed, thick cotton canvas 30L cooler bag is the perfect addition to any summer picnic, or to transport chilled goods for a big day out. With on-trend natural tones, and faux leather trims, this cooler bag offers both style and sophistication. The bag is large enough to fit all your chilled goods plus more and features a conveniently located front pocket. It also has two sturdy carry handles one with an ergonomic dome-fastened grip, and the other a detachable and adjustable shoulder strap. The water-resistant PEVA/aluminium foil inner liner makes it easy to wipe clean, and the twin zip provides a wide opening for ease of use. The bag is presented with a Keepsake hangtag.
