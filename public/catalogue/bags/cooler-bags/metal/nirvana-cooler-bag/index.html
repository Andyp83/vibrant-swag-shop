Nirvana Cooler Bag

PLU 115113 · Cooler Bags · Metal

Nirvana is where fashion meets function and this modern 13L cooler bag has all the hallmarks of impeccable style and superior quality. It has thick PE foam insulation encased in a water-resistant PEVA inner liner. Nirvana can carry a six pack of beer or soda. The outer construction is poly-canvas with an on-trend heather-style finish. Nirvana has an adjustable shoulder strap with a handy metal bottle opener riveted to it. Other features include a carry handle, a slip pocket on the front and a top closure with a smart electroplated silver zip.
