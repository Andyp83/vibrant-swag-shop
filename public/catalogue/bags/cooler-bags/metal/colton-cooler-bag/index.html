Colton Cooler Bag

PLU 121856 · Cooler Bags · Metal

Impressive 19L cooler bag with PE foam insulation encased in a PEVA inner liner. The outer construction is natural 100% 450gsm cotton canvas with stunning leather-look PU accents. It has a woven carry handle on the top and a woven shoulder strap which is adjustable and removable via metal clips. Other features include a zippered top closure, piping details to hold the structure and a large front slip pocket.
