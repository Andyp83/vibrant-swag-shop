SPICE Waste2Gear Neo Cooler

PLU 127246 · Cooler Bags · Metal

SPICE strive to create extraordinary products that have a positive impact on the planet. Their Waste2Gear range uses recovered ocean plastics, which are gathered, cleaned, and converted into fibres and woven into long-lasting fabric. The Neo Cooler bag is water-resistant, 100% carbon neutral to delivery and features waterproof zippers and a 15L capacity. Lightweight and stylish, the Waste2Gear Neo Cooler bag is perfect for keeping food and drink cool while you are on the go.
