Aquinas 20L Duffle Bag

PLU 121426 · Duffle Bags · Plastic

Superior large duffle bag which is manufactured from polycanvas with a water-resistant PU coating and decorative black zips and piping. It has strong woven carry handles with a Velcro cuff and a woven shoulder strap that is adjustable and removable. Aquinas has three zippered external pockets, two of which are shoe bags that can also be used for wet gym gear etc. Other features include a sturdy internal baseboard and strong external plastic feet.
