ARCHER Tasman Duffle Bag

PLU 128648 · Duffle Bags · Fabric & Textile

Designed for life in motion, the ARCHER Tasman Duffle Bag pairs urban style with everyday practicality. Made from water-resistant washed nylon and featuring water-resistant zips, it offers a generous 38L main compartment with a padded sleeve for devices up to 17", and large pockets to keep your essentials organised. Additional external front and back zippered pockets provide extra storage, while the woven carry handles and adjustable woven shoulder strap with padding ensure comfort on the go. The padded gusset provides added protection for belongings while reinforced zippers provide structure and durability. Finished with a unique textured fabric, ARCHER branded lining, and presented with an ARCHER branded hangtag.
