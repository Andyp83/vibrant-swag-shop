ARCHER Galileo Duffle Bag

PLU 128643 · Duffle Bags · Fabric & Textile

Designed for life in motion, the ARCHER Galileo Duffle Bag combines modern styling with practical versatility. Made from durable polyester with a subtle heathered finish, it features a spacious 36L zippered main compartment with a separate padded device sleeve. An additional back zippered pocket, dedicated shoe compartment, and a range of internal pockets keep essentials organised. The detachable, adjustable shoulder strap makes carrying a breeze and it also features a handy luggage strap for easy travel. Finished with colour-match zips, ARCHER branded lining, and presented with an ARCHER branded hangtag.
