Swiss Peak Weekend or Sport Bag

PLU 109994 · Duffle Bags · Fabric & Textile

Swiss Peak have combined smart design with the needs of a busy lifestyle to create this versatile duffle bag, ideal for long weekends or as a sports or gym bag. Manufactured from durable 600D and 1680D polyester it features a spacious main compartment with a U-shaped zip opening for easy access, along with a side-entry shoe compartment that includes an air vent to keep things fresh.
