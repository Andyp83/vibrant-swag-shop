Swiss Peak Toiletry Bag

PLU 118872 · Toiletry Bags · Fabric & Textile

The thoughtfully designed Swiss Peak Toiletry Bag is a luxurious travel accessory with the perfect balance of functionality and simplicity. It is manufactured from polyester and can sit upright or be hung up by a stowable hook for even easier access. The bag has a large zip-around opening with conveniently looped zipper pulls, a zippered side pocket and a woven carry handle. It has a large internal compartment which features a unique, magnetically sealed toothbrush pocket along with three mesh pockets and two slip pockets which keep items organised and clean. The Swiss Peak logo is subtly branded on the front of the bag.
