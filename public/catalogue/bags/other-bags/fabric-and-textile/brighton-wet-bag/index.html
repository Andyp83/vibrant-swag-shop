Brighton Wet Bag

PLU 115963 · Other Bags · Fabric & Textile

The Brighton Wet Bag is the perfect way to store wet or dirty items. It is suitable for nappies, dirty clothes, swimsuits, gym gear etc. Brighton is manufactured from water-resistant 1680D high density pongee ripstop and has an easy to wipe lining. Other features include a zippered closure and a domed strap which is great for attaching it to bags or pram handles etc.
