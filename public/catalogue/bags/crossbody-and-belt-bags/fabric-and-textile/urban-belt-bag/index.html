Urban Belt Bag

PLU 120259 · Crossbody & Belt Bags · Fabric & Textile

The Urban Belt Bag from XD Design combines streetwear with security, resulting in a stylish anti-theft bag that can be worn as a belt bag, across the body or over the shoulder and will keep belongings safe on the go. The bag uses anti-cut material and features a lockable zipper, a safe 3-point buckle opening on the adjustable strap and slip pockets with RIFD protection.
