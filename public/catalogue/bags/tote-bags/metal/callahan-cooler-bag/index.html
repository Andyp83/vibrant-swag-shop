Callahan Cooler Bag

PLU 129435 · Tote Bags · Metal

A spacious 26L cooler bag designed to keep contents cool and fresh while on the go. Crafted from durable 340gsm, 100% natural cotton and features PE insulation and an aluminium foil lining. The natural cotton body has reinforced self-fabric panels and a generous gusset for extra strength and capacity. Choose from a wide range of handle colours and colour match your print for cohesive, standout branding. Perfect for grocery runs, beach trips, or picnics. Production lead time is 14 - 16 weeks on this product. Sampling requires additional lead time.
