Aquinas Sling Laptop Bag

PLU 121424 · Laptop Bags · Fabric & Textile

Sturdy laptop bag with padded compartments for both a laptop and a tablet which is manufactured from polycanvas with a water-resistant PU coating. It has a small zippered pocket on the front, woven carry handles with a Velcro cuff, and an adjustable woven shoulder strap. Aquinas effortlessly transforms from a satchel to a slinger bag by pulling out the shoulder strap from its pocket.
