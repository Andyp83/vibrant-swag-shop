ARCHER Galileo Messenger Bag

PLU 128645 · Laptop Bags · Fabric & Textile

Designed for life in motion, the ARCHER Galileo Messenger Bag is a modern take on the classic messenger bag blending clean design with smart functionality. Made from durable polyester with a subtle heathered finish, it features a zippered main compartment with a padded pocket to fit devices up to 16". Internal zippered compartments and handy organising pockets keep your tech essentials in place. Designed for versatility, it includes a detachable, adjustable shoulder strap balanced for both crossbody and one-shoulder wear. Finished with colour-match zips, ARCHER branded lining, and presented with an ARCHER branded hangtag.
