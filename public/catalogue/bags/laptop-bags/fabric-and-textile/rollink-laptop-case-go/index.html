Rollink Laptop Case Go

PLU 127625 · Laptop Bags · Fabric & Textile

The Rollink Laptop Case GO is designed for those who value security, convenience, and style. Its durable, slash and cut resistant hard shell provides an extra layer of protection, safeguarding valuables from damage and unauthorised access. Inside, multiple compartments keep essentials like credit cards, keys, and small change neatly organized and within reach. Crafted from water-resistant materials, this case ensures belongings stay dry in unexpected weather. An adjustable strap offers a comfortable fit, allowing it to be worn over the neck or as a crossbody. Thoughtfully packaged in a non-woven bag and a sleek craft cardboard gift box, it's a refined essential for everyday carry.
