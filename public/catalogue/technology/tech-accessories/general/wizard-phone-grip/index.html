Wizard Phone Grip

PLU 117179 · Tech Accessories · General

Unique phone grip with a compact 10mm profile that adheres to the back of a phone or phone case and pops out to provide a secure finger grip. It slides comfortably between two fingers and significantly reduces the risk of dropping a phone while using it. Wizard helps to take better selfies and can be used as a phone stand. It can also be used as a cable tidy for wrapping earbuds or a charging cable around.
