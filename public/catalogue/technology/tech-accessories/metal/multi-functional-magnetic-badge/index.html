Multi-Functional Magnetic Badge

PLU 134170 · Tech Accessories · Metal

The Multi-Functional Magnetic Badge is a versatile promotional item with a wide range of applications. Stick it to a fridge, attach it to a phone or use it as a supporter badge. The included metal ring allows it to be used as a name or button badge by placing the ring inside your shirt and attaching the badge magnetically on the outside. It also makes it compatible with non-MagSafe phones. Presented in a kraft paper sleeve.
