Sticky Screen Cleaner

PLU 108268 · Screen Cleaners · Fabric & Textile

Compact microfibre screen cleaner which sticks securely to the back of a phone when not in use. It has an adhesive backing that remains sticky for many uses and can be washed and reused. Sticky Screen Cleaner is ideal for cleaning phones, cameras and any other devices with small screens. It can be branded in spot colours or stunning full colour and is available in custom shapes at no extra cost but there is a size restriction. The screen cleaner is mounted on a card with instructions for use. A production lead time of 20 working days applies to this product by airfreight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost.
