NATURA Bamboo Wireless Fast Charging Hub

PLU 126409 · Wireless Chargers · Paper & Cardboard

Charge up to three devices at once using the 15W wireless charger with fast charging capability, which doubles as a conventional charging hub. The charging hub features a natural bamboo casing, and fast charging is easily enabled for compatible devices when used with a 20W+ Quick Charge or Power Delivery mains adaptor. Twin USB-A ports allow for conventional charging, and the hub is presented in natural kraft gift box with NATURA branding.
