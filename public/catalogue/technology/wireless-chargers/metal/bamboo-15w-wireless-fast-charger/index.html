Bamboo 15W Wireless Fast Charger

PLU 125555 · Wireless Chargers · Metal

Handy 15W wireless charger with fast charging capability. The charger features a natural bamboo charging pad and an aluminium base. An outer ring illuminates to indicate charging status. For convenient wireless charging, simply plug it into any USB port or mains adapter and place a compatible device on the bamboo charging pad. The charger will work with most modern devices and is presented in a printed black gift box with instructions.
