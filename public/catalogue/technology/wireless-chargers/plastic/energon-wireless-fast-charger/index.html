Energon Wireless Fast Charger

PLU 125556 · Wireless Chargers · Plastic

Handy 15W wireless charger with fast charging capability. The charger features a large charging pad and an LED light to indicate charging status. For convenient wireless charging, simply plug it into any USB port or mains adapter and place a compatible device on the charging pad. The charger includes a 100cm Type-C cable and will work with most modern devices. Energon is presented in a printed black gift box with instructions.
