NATURA Bamboo Solar Power Bank

PLU 123651 · Power Banks · Paper & Cardboard

Versatile 10,000mAh power bank with solar charging capability and natural bamboo casing with a handy LED power gauge. It has Micro-B and Type C 5V/2A inputs and a 5V/2A USB-A output. The power bank will recharge in 4-5 hours via USB or a mains adaptor and has built-in solar panels for charging in emergencies. The power bank is presented in natural kraft gift box with NATURA branding. A Micro-B USB cable and instructions are included.
