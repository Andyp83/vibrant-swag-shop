Bolt 22.5W QC Power Bank

PLU 124864 · Power Banks · Metal

High performance 20,000mAh power bank that delivers up to 22.5W QC and 18W PD quick charge capability. Bolt is capable of charging up to three devices simultaneously and features a digital power gauge to show the power percentage remaining. The power bank has Micro-B and Type-C inputs with a Type-C and two USB-A outputs. This impressive power bank is presented in a printed black gift box. An optional EVA carry case and a triple connector cable are available on request.
