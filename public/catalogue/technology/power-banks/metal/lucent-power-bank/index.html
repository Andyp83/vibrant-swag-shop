Lucent Power Bank

PLU 200312 · Power Banks · Metal

Compact 1200mAh emergency power bank with a handy carabiner and a three function LED light. It will charge almost any smartphone and has built-in cables with a Type-C connector and a unique double-sided connector with Lightning on one side and Micro-B USB on the other. Lucent has an input power of 5V/1A, an output power of 5V/1A and a handy LED power gauge. It can be recharged from a USB port or a mains adaptor. Lucent is presented in a white box with a Micro-B USB cable for charging and an instruction leaflet. An optional EVA carry case is available on request.
