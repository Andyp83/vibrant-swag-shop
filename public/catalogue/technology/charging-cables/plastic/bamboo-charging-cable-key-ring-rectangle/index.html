Bamboo Charging Cable Key Ring - Rectangle

PLU 121410 · Charging Cables · Plastic

A handy mobile charging cable key ring for phones, tablets, cameras, GPS etc. It offers three different connectors in one compact bamboo unit, a USB, Type-C, and a Lightening that can be flipped over to be a Micro-B. This device is for charging only and is not designed for data connections. Bamboo is a natural material and because the plant is fast growing, it places less demands on natural resources and produces a material that is durable and versatile. Bamboo can also produce unavoidable variances in the grain pattern, colour and branding. It laser engraves to a natural etch.
