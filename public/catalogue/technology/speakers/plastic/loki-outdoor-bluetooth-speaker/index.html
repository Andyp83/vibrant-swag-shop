Loki Outdoor Bluetooth Speaker

PLU 121666 · Speakers · Plastic

Compact 5W Outdoor Bluetooth speaker which is compatible with most mobile devices that support Bluetooth. Manufactured from ABS and Thermoplastic polyurethane, Loki is IPX7 water-resistant and is perfect to take on all adventures. It has a wireless connectivity range of over 10 metres and a playing time of approximately 3 hours depending on the volume. It is easily recharged from a laptop, a power bank or a USB mains adapter and it is supplied with a Type-C USB cable and an instruction manual.
