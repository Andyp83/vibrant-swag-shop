Beatcore Bluetooth Speaker

PLU 125539 · Speakers · Plastic

Premium water-resistant Bluetooth speaker with a hard-wearing design, perfect for outdoor use. Beatcore has two 6W speaker outputs, and music can be played for up to five hours via Bluetooth, AUX or Micro-SD card. The speaker has music, volume and hands-free controls and a built-in light display at either end of the speaker showcases the two branding areas. The speaker recharges in three hours and is presented in a printed black gift box with instructions and a charging cable.
