Nuvo Magnetic Phone Holder

PLU 112831 · Stands & Holders · Metal

Magnetic phone holder that attaches securely to a car air vent with a spring-loaded clip to enable the phone to be used as a GPS. To establish the magnetic connection, the thin metal plate (included) is attached to the phone or phone case with strong 3M adhesive that wont leave marks if it is removed. The metal plate attachment is a proven concept and will not damage or affect the performance of the phone.
