SOLS Slam Kids Hooded Sweatshirt

PLU 120671 · Sweatshirts · Fabric & Textile

Versatile 280gsm fleece-lined kid's hooded sweatshirt which is manufactured from a 50/50 blend of ring-spun cotton and polyester with ribbed elastane on the hem and cuffs. It has set in sleeves and a large kangaroo pocket on the front. The brushed fleece lining gives the garment a soft and cosy feel while adding extra warmth. It is a European fit and is available in five sizes.
