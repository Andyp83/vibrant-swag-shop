SOLS Stone Unisex Hooded Sweatshirt

PLU 119998 · Sweatshirts · Fabric & Textile

Stylish 260gsm, unisex adult sweatshirt with a full zippered front. It is manufactured from a 50/50 blend of ring-spun cotton and polyester with ribbed elastane on the hem and cuffs. Other features include set in sleeves, two kangaroo pockets on the front and a hood with a tone-on-tone drawstring. Stone has a straight cut design and a brushed fleece lining that gives it a soft and cosy feel while adding extra warmth. The garment is cut and sewn with a European fit and is available in seven sizes. It provides an excellent branding opportunity that will appeal to a broad market.
