SOLS Surf Kids Windbreaker

PLU 120672 · Jackets · General

Lightweight unlined children's wind breaker which is a larger fit to accommodate the layers underneath. It is manufactured from 210T polyamide and is showerproof and wind-resistant. The jacket has a full zippered front and a high collar which includes a stowaway elasticated hood. The waist can be adjusted using drawstrings for added protection from the elements. Other features include zippered side pockets and elastic cuffs. The surf windbreaker is available in five sizes.
