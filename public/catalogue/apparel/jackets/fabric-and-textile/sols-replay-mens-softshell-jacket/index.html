SOLS Replay Men's Softshell Jacket

PLU 120006 · Jackets · Fabric & Textile

Versatile men's 340gsm, long sleeve softshell jacket which is manufactured from a blend of polyester and elastane which is wind-resistant and has a waterproof rating of 8,000mm (waterproof in light conditions). The jacket has triple-layer insulation with a cosy fleece lining, and it is designed as smart outdoor casual wear for all seasons. It has a full zippered front which extends to include the high collar, a hood with a drawstring, two zippered side pockets which double as hand warmers, one large internal zippered pocket and one zippered chest pocket. Other features include a longer back panel, adjustable cuffs with a self-gripping fastener and an adjustable elastic waist closure for additional warmth. Replay is a European fit and is available in six sizes.
