TRENDSWEAR Milford Womens Puffer Jacket

PLU 125968 · Jackets · Fabric & Textile

The Milford Women's Puffer Jacket is made from midweight nylon, featuring a soft outer shell and lining. The 180gsm insulation is made from 100% GRS (Global Recycled Standard) certified polyester, and along with an adjustable hood and concealed knitted cuffs, this jacket provides cosy and dependable protection. The fabric and filler are OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified, having been independently tested for harmful substances and proven safe for skin contact. Available in seven Australasian sizes, with the choice of coloured zipper pullers from the standard colour range.
