SOLS Roxy Women's Softshell Jacket

PLU 118090 · Jackets · Fabric & Textile

Versatile women's 340gsm softshell jacket with triple-layer insulation and a fleece backing for extra warmth. It is manufactured from a blend of polyester and elastane which is wind-resistant and has a waterproof rating of 8,000mm (waterproof in light conditions). Roxy has two large vertical zippered side pockets which double as hand warmers, a vertical zippered chest pocket and a large zippered internal pocket. It features a longer back panel and an adjustable elastic waist closure for additional warmth. Other features include adjustable Velcro cuff closures, an elastic loop on the collar to keep a headphone cable tidy and two elastic loops on the back for hanging the jacket up. Roxy is a European fit and is available in five sizes, combining fashion with function to produce an outstanding all weather garment.
