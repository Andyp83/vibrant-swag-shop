SOLS Wilson Men's Puffer Jacket

PLU 120016 · Jackets · Fabric & Textile

Lightweight men's 380T, long sleeve puffer jacket with a polyamide outer and lining, and a blend of 100% recycled polyester padding for extra warmth. The jacket has a full zippered front which extends to include the high collar, two large, zippered side pockets which double as hand warmers, and two internal pockets. Wilson is a European fit and is available in six sizes. An in-bag folding system with stoppers is included for convenient storage.
