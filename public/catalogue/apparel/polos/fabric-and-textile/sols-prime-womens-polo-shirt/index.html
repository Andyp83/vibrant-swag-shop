SOLS Prime Women's Polo Shirt

PLU 118088 · Polos · Fabric & Textile

Smart cut and sewn 200gsm women's short-sleeved poly-cotton polo shirt in a pique weave. It is manufactured from a 65/35 blend of polyester and ring-spun cotton. Other features include ribbed cuffs and collar, reinforced shoulder seams, neck tape reinforcing, a tone-on-tone three button placket in the neckline and a spare button on the side seam. Prime is a European fit and is available in five sizes. It is a versatile garment that is suitable for corporate or casual workwear as well as sport and leisure.
