Custom Mens Sports Zipped Hoodie

PLU 121166 · Teamwear · Fabric & Textile

Stylish men's hoodie with a full zippered front. It is manufactured from 280gsm, 100% polyester brushed fleece with knitted hem and cuffs which are available in the choice of 19 colours. Other features include long sleeves, one kangaroo pocket at the front and a hood with a drawstring. The hoodie has a straight cut design and a jersey lining that gives it a cosy feel. The garment is cut and sewn with a regular fit and is available in seven sizes which can be branded edge to edge in spot colours or full-colour print. Personalisation e.g. names and numbers are available free of charge. A production lead time of 40 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost. Custom labels are available upon request free of charge.
