Custom Varsity Jacket

PLU 121170 · Teamwear · Fabric & Textile

Brushed fleece lined unisex custom varsity jacket, which is manufactured from 280gsm, 100% polyester jersey. The jacket is a regular fit with a snap button front closure. The knitted hem and cuffs provide an adjusted fit to those areas. Other features include two side welt pockets and a knitted curve collar. The hem, cuffs and collar are available in a choice of 19 colours. It is available in seven sizes which can be branded edge to edge in spot colours or full-colour print. Personalisation e.g. names and numbers are available free of charge. A production lead time of 40 working days applies to this product by air freight and larger orders can be sea freighted on request to reduce the freight cost. Custom labels are available upon request free of charge.
