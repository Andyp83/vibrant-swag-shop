TRENDSWEAR Element Unisex T-Shirt

PLU 122452 · T-Shirts · Fabric & Textile

The Element Unisex T-Shirt is crafted from lightweight 150gsm combed cotton, which is soft against the skin and breathable. The 1x1 ribbed neckline and shoulder to shoulder bias binding provide a structured look while improving durability and comfort. The fabric is OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified, having been independently tested for harmful substances and proven safe for skin contact. Available in eight Australasian sizes, the Element Unisex T-Shirt has been built for comfort and designed for movement.
