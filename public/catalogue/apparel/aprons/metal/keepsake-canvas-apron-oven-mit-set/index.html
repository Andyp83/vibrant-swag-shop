Keepsake Canvas Apron & Oven Mit Set

PLU 133819 · Aprons · Metal

Cook in style with the Keepsake Canvas Apron and Oven Mitt Set, both crafted from 300gsm cotton canvas in a washed black finish. The apron features a leather-look PU front pocket and trim, with gunmetal rivets securing natural cotton straps that clip at the top and thread at the waist for an adjustable fit. The oven mitt's black-and-white striped lining and brown PU hanging loop make for a practical yet polished companion. Both pieces feature a small woven label with Keepsake logo and are presented with a Keepsake branded hangtag.
